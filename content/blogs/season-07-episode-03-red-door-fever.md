The clinic board changed while a nurse was reading it aloud.

`ROOM 4 READY` became a red door.

The nurse stopped mid-number. The kitten beside her mother hugged a paper form to her chest. Shadow, who had been watching the reflection in the glass, raised one paw before anyone touched the board.

"It changed from inside the page cabinet," he said.

Jinx drew a square in her notebook and colored it red.

### More Doors

The second door appeared on a weather panel. The third appeared above a library desk. The fourth flashed on a school lunch counter and vanished before the line moved.

Pixel wanted to chase the doors. Jinx wanted to count them first.

"If we run after every red mark, we will miss the pattern," she said.

Shadow checked the back of the clinic board. No broken glass. No new sticker. No loose wire. The surface looked innocent.

"Same cabinet family," he said.

Jinx added three lines to her map and circled the shared cabinet mark. "The old repair notice was not about one sign."

### The Red Clerk Watches

At the archive window, the Red Clerk watched a permit board repaint itself.

"It should have stayed local," he said.

Whiskers heard the word `should` and wrote it down.

Cipher arrived with a stack of status cards. "We know the red doors are symptoms. We do not know every affected board yet."

"We know enough to isolate the cabinet family," Jinx said.

The Red Clerk gripped the window frame. "If you isolate it, half the district loses live notices."

"If we do nothing," Shadow said, "the notices are already lying."

BL4CK4T's pawprint appeared on a blank strip at the bottom of the clinic board.

`A PUBLIC DOOR NEEDS PUBLIC CARE.`

The board went dark, then returned with one plain line: `USE PAPER FORMS UNTIL VERIFIED.`

The line moved before anyone found a speech.

Jinx marked the first useful change on her map.

## Teaching Tie-In

- Concept: exposed services.
- Story idea: the red doors spread across public boards that many people rely on.
- Key distinction: a hidden flaw becomes more urgent when the affected system faces the public.
- Defensive habit: identify exposed systems and isolate affected groups before symptoms spread farther.
- Season thread: maintenance is part of trust.
