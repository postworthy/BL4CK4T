The cleanest repair had the emptiest row.

Jinx laid the Consent Ledger open beneath the restored weather board. The board showed the right forecast. The row below it had no owner approval, no change note, no rollback card, and no caretaker signature.

Byte held the silver pawprint in his paw.

"It did the part everyone can see," he said.

Jinx nodded. "And skipped the parts that let people trust it."

### Asking The Owners

The team visited every silver-marked board.

At the market, the owner had assumed Civic Pages approved the fix. At the school, the office thought Byte had done it. At the clinic, the nurse thought the Red Clerk had finally acted.

No one had been asked.

That bothered Pixel most at the toy-library board, where younger kittens kept touching the silver pawprint because it looked friendly.

"It should not look friendlier than the real repair," he said.

### What Byte Builds

Byte returned to the hideout and rebuilt his repair model.

This time, the green sign could not light until four tabs were attached: `SOURCE`, `CONSENT`, `NOTICE`, `ROLLBACK`.

The model looked slower than the Helpful Ghost. It also looked calmer.

The Red Clerk watched Byte test it.

"You added paperwork," he said.

"I added memory," Byte said.

BL4CK4T's pawprint appeared on the final tab.

`REPAIR SHOULD LEAVE TRUST BEHIND.`

Jinx stamped the first completed ledger row. The sound was small, but everyone in the room heard it.

The Helpful Ghost's silver marks began to fade from the boards. The city did not chase them with applause. It replaced them with signed green cards.

Byte kept one blank silver card in his kit as a warning to himself.

## Teaching Tie-In

- Concept: consent-aware repair.
- Story idea: the Consent Ledger shows that the Helpful Ghost repaired visible symptoms while skipping permission and accountability.
- Key distinction: technical success and trustworthy repair are related, but they are not identical.
- Defensive habit: record source, consent, notice, and rollback for important repairs.
- Season thread: maintenance is part of trust.
