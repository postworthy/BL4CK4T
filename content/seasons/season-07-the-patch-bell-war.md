The Patch Bell War begins with one red card that stayed red after the Season 6 flood ended. The Script Kitties discover that the problem was not new noise, but an old known repair that waited too long. Season 7 turns patching, rollback, verified updates, and consent-aware repair into a Cybertropolis story about caring for public systems before the city has to learn the lesson under pressure.
