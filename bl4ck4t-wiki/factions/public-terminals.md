# Public Terminals

## Summary

Public Terminals is the civic desk responsible for terminals used by the public across libraries, museums, and learning spaces.

## Story Use

Use Public Terminals when a case involves shared machines outside a single lab.

Season 7 expands this role: Public Terminals coordinates with local owners when desk terminals, public workstations, checkout stands, clinic intake stations, or school work surfaces need verified repair through the City Runtime update track.

## Canon Notes

- Public Terminals does not own every endpoint's local context.
- It should coordinate with Library Systems and Museum Systems.
- Public Terminals can help identify affected desk terminals, but local teams still know which forms, classes, counters, or services those terminals support.
