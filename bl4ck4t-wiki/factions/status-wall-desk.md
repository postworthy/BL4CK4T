# Status Wall Desk

## Summary

Status Wall Desk is a Season 6 civic group connected to honest status, triage, and resilient service design.

## Story Use

Use this group when the story needs caretakers, clerks, builders, or public helpers who make the season's response work at city scale.

## Canon Notes

- The group supports the Script Kitties but does not replace them.
- The group should keep a distinct function in later seasons.
