# First Bell Desk

## Summary

The First Bell Desk is the standing Cybertropolis coordination point created after the Copycat Sprite incident. Its job is to receive lab incident reports, assign owners, and keep response messages moving.

## Season 3 Use

- Grimalkin proposes it during the response.
- The finale shows it becoming a city practice.
- It gives later seasons a credible reporting and coordination path.

## Continuity Notes

The First Bell Desk complements the Glass Bureau. The Glass Bureau handles cross-grid evidence and civic reports; the First Bell Desk handles fast lab incident coordination.

## Published Canon Details

- Created after the Copycat Sprite incident.
- Uses a brass bell, response binder, map drawer, first report form, status update form, and recovery check form.
- Represents calm coordination, not panic.
