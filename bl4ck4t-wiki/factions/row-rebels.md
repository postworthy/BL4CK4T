# Row Rebels

## Summary

The Row Rebels are a mixed-motive Signal Row youth group tied to Echo Grid curiosity, Crunch Charm rumor culture, public protest, status, and disruption.

## Story Use

Use the Row Rebels when a story needs young people who are right about being ignored but wrong about how harmless their spectacle is.

## Canon Notes

- Rook is the most visible named Row Rebels figure.
- The group is not a villain crew.
- Future stories should keep their motives mixed.
