# City Chronicle

## Summary

The City Chronicle is Cybertropolis's public student news desk. In Season 4 it amplifies the Vanishing Caller story before the evidence is settled.

## Story Use

Use the Chronicle to show how real concern can become overconfident public narrative. It should not be evil; it is fast, competitive, and tempted by dramatic names.

## Canon Notes

- Chronicle headlines can raise stakes by spreading partial stories.
- Jinx should challenge Chronicle claims with evidence, not contempt.
- The Chronicle can learn from the season and publish a correction or fuller account.
