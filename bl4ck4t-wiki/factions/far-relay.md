# Far Relay

## Summary

The Far Relay is a shadowy broker layer where stolen project notes, old access passes, and system rumors move between crews. It gives Season 2 a wider adversary network without requiring a single mastermind behind every clue.

## Story Use

Use the Far Relay when the investigation leaves the Ledger Lab and reveals that the mismatch is part of a wider pattern. It should appear through records, marks, rumors, and traded fragments rather than direct exposition.

## Inspiration Class

Brokered access, underground information markets, espionage-adjacent data trading, and rumor networks. Keep the public version fictional and transformed.

## Canon Notes

- The Far Relay is a layer, not one person.
- It should not overshadow Jinx's investigation.
- The strongest Season 2 antagonist remains ignored evidence plus brokered misuse.
