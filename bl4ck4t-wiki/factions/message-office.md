# Message Office

## Summary

The Message Office is the Cybertropolis desk that handles public message routing, class lists, notices, and mailroom reports.

## Story Use

Use the Message Office in Season 5 to show how ordinary trusted message systems become part of incident response. It is a helper institution, not a careless failure point.

## Canon Notes

- The Message Office works with the First Bell Desk during suspicious-message incidents.
- It can quarantine and warn, but it needs clear reports from affected readers.
- It should remain distinct from the City Chronicle, which publishes news.
