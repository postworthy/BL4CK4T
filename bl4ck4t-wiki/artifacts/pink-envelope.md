# Pink Envelope

## Published Canon

The Pink Envelope appears at the end of Season 4 on the Notice Wall, addressed to `everybody who wants to be liked`. It opens the Season 5 social-lure thread.

## Story Function

- Turns curiosity and belonging into the first Season 5 pressure point.
- Carries the love-letter theme without using real-world malware details.
- Gives Cipher and Jinx a visible object to quarantine before anyone opens it.

## Current State

Season 5 begins with the sealed envelope under caretaker review. It should not become an ordinary letter after the season; it is the origin artifact for the Glitter Letter incident.
