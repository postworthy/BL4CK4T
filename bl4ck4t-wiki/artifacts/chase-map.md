# Chase Map

## Published Canon

The Chase Map is Jinx and Shadow's Season 4 trace board. It tracks confirmed calls, likely paths, ruled-out routes, open questions, and public claims.

## Story Function

- Makes trace evidence visible.
- Prevents the team from turning rumor into certainty.
- Lets Shadow contribute physical route clues while Jinx guards the knowledge state.

## Current State

Introduced for Season 4 as a private investigation tool connected to the First Bell Desk and Glass Bureau.
