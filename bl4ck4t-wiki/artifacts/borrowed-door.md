# Borrowed Door

## Published Canon

The Borrowed Door is the Script Kitties' Season 2 phrase for familiar access appearing in unfamiliar behavior. It names defender-facing evidence without teaching unauthorized access.

## Story Function

- Turns account misuse into a readable metaphor.
- Helps the team discuss misuse without over-claiming identity or intent.

## Current State

Reusable phrase for defender-side account behavior, especially when records show mismatched timing, location, or ownership.
