# Quarantine Tray

## Published Canon

The Quarantine Tray is the Season 5 containment tool for suspicious messages and attachments. It lets the team hold a message for review without opening it in normal city mail paths.

## Story Function

- Gives safe handling a physical shape.
- Supports Pixel's curiosity without letting curiosity drive unsafe action.
- Connects First Bell Desk response to message-specific containment.

## Current State

Introduced in Season 5 as a reusable defensive artifact for future suspicious-message stories.
