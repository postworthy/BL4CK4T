# Little Blue Pawprint

## Published Canon

The Little Blue Pawprint is a handmade zine and sticker signal that spreads the Echo Grid rumor through Packet Market and Signal Row. It turns scattered listener knowledge into public culture before the team fully understands the system, copying the sound without preserving the difference between public messages and routing behavior.

## Story Function

- Transforms phreak-era public sharing and lore into a fictional youth culture artifact.
- Shows that partial knowledge can mutate when it becomes fashionable.

## Current State

Season 1 historical-culture artifact. It can recur as nostalgia, archive material, or a warning about incomplete public explanations.

## Technical Texture Notes

- Safe terms for future references: public message, routing behavior, system context, partial explanation, rumor path.
- The zine should be exciting and incomplete, not a real instruction document.
- Its canon lesson is that partial technical knowledge can become risky when context is stripped away.
