# Glitter Letter

## Published Canon

The Glitter Letter is the Season 5 message lure. It looks personal and friendly, but it tries to copy itself through trusted message paths after a reader opens it.

## Story Function

- Makes social malware visible without showing real code.
- Connects affection, curiosity, and address-book trust to spread.
- Lets the team teach safe handling, reporting, and quarantine.

## Current State

Introduced in Season 5. Treat it as an incident artifact, not a reusable toy or tool.
