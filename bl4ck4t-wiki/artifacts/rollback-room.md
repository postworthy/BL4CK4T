# Rollback Room

## Published Canon

Draft canon until Season 7 publication. The Rollback Room is where caretakers prepare recovery plans before applying risky or urgent repairs.

## Story Function

- Shows that responsible patching includes a way back if repair fails.
- Gives the Red Clerk a credible fear rooted in a past broken update.
- Helps Cipher distinguish responsible emergency action from reckless action.

## Visual Motifs

- Shelves of labeled restore cards.
- Clock faces that can be turned back only with approval.
- Worktables with before-and-after diagrams.
