# Crown Jewel Register

## Published Canon

Crown Jewel Register is the Season 8 civic artifact for The Aurora Vault. It makes crown-jewel protection, access review, and quiet anomaly reading visible to the team and to Cybertropolis.

## Story Function

- Gives the season lesson a physical form.
- Tests Jinx's growth under pressure.
- Creates a reusable civic practice after the incident closes.

## Current State

Introduced during Season 8. Future stories may reuse it as precedent, but they should not reopen the same incident without a new cause.
