# Service Map

## Published Canon

Service Map is the Season 6 civic artifact for The Day The City Would Not Answer. It makes honest status, triage, and resilient service design visible to the team and to Cybertropolis.

## Story Function

- Gives the season lesson a physical form.
- Tests Whiskers's growth under pressure.
- Creates a reusable civic practice after the incident closes.

## Current State

Introduced during Season 6. Future stories may reuse it as precedent, but they should not reopen the same incident without a new cause.
