# Service Map

## Published Canon

The Service Map is a Season 6 civic-defense artifact that shows what depends on what during a service disruption. It includes systems, desks, queues, fallback paths, caretakers, and the people waiting on the services.

## Story Function

- Makes availability visible.
- Helps Jinx map dependencies rather than only clues.
- Helps Whiskers prioritize communication and service restoration.
- Shows that a stalled service affects real people, not only machines.

## Current State

Introduced during the Season 6 import. It should become the season's central artifact and later remain available for resilience, outage, and continuity stories.
