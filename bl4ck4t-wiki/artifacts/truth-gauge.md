# Truth Gauge

## Published Canon

Truth Gauge is the Season 9 civic artifact for The Machine That Lied. It makes independent sensing, manual checks, and safety-first response visible to the team and to Cybertropolis.

## Story Function

- Gives the season lesson a physical form.
- Tests Shadow's growth under pressure.
- Creates a reusable civic practice after the incident closes.

## Current State

Introduced during Season 9. Future stories may reuse it as precedent, but they should not reopen the same incident without a new cause.
