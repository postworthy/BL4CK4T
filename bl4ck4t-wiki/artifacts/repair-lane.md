# Repair Lane

## Published Canon

The Repair Lane is the trusted civic path for verified updates, caretaker approval, rollback planning, and public repair communication.

## Story Function

- Separates legitimate urgent repair from rumor and imitation.
- Gives citizens a place to check whether an update notice is real.
- Carries City Runtime repair cards to desk terminals, public boards, and service-room machines.
- Lets Byte build repair tooling that respects consent.
- Contrasts directly with the Helpful Ghost.

## Visual Motifs

- A lit lane through the Civic Pages District.
- Green verification lamps.
- Repair cards stamped by the caretaker who authorized them.

## Current State

Introduced during Season 7. It becomes the approved alternative to both silent patch delay and unauthorized repair.
