# Patch Bell

## Published Canon

Patch Bell is the Season 7 civic artifact for The Patch Bell War. It makes patch, isolate, rollback, and consent-aware emergency repair visible to the team and to Cybertropolis.

## Story Function

- Gives the season lesson a physical form.
- Tests Cipher's growth under pressure.
- Creates a reusable civic practice after the incident closes.

## Current State

Introduced during Season 7. Future stories may reuse it as precedent, but they should not reopen the same incident without a new cause.
