# Notice Wall

## Published Canon

The Notice Wall is a public board where Season 4 rumors, warnings, Chronicle clips, and anonymous notes gather faster than the evidence can settle.

## Story Function

- Turns public mythology into something visible.
- Gives Jinx a place to separate claims, guesses, and facts.
- Shows how one dramatic phrase can outrun careful reporting.

## Current State

Introduced for Season 4. It should remain a public rumor surface, not an official incident system.
