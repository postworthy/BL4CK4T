# Device Census

## Published Canon

Device Census is the Season 10 civic artifact for The Army Of Little Eyes. It makes inventory, safer defaults, education, and manufacturer responsibility visible to the team and to Cybertropolis.

## Story Function

- Gives the season lesson a physical form.
- Tests Pixel's growth under pressure.
- Creates a reusable civic practice after the incident closes.
