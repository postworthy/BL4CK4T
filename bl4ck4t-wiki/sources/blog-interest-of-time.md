# The Interest Of Time

## Summary

BL4CK4T sends the Script Kitties a self-discovery quest using a neon simulator for simple and compound interest. The lesson ties math, patience, debt, consistency, and character growth together.

## Canon Contributions

- BL4CK4T can send learning environments through holographic or virtual city interfaces.
- The team can split into individual panels/challenges, each revealing a character-specific insight.
- Grimalkin values planning and early starts.
- Pixel experiments with compounding frequency.

## Lesson Contributions

- Compound interest earns on principal and previous interest.
- Time is often more important than starting size.
- Debt compounds against the learner.
- The Rule of 72 estimates doubling time.
- Math lessons can connect to identity and future planning.
