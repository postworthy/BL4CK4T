# WannaCry Ransomware

## Summary

WannaCry was a major 2017 ransomware outbreak associated with worm-like spread and a known Microsoft vulnerability. The durable lesson is that patching, segmentation, backups, and preparation reduce damage.

## Defensive Lesson

- Patch known exploited vulnerabilities quickly.
- Keep reliable offline or protected backups.
- Segment networks so one failure does not become every failure.
- Practice response before an incident.
