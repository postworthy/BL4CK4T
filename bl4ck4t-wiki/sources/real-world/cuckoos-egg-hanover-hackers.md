# The Cuckoo's Egg And The Hanover Hackers

## Purpose

Source page for the real-world historical material behind Season 2: Cliff Stoll's investigation at Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory, the 75-cent accounting discrepancy, the intrusion trail through academic and defense-connected systems, and the later Hanover hacker/KGB espionage case.

## Extraction Notes

- The strongest source for technical chronology is Stoll's 1988 CACM article.
- The strongest source for legal outcome in the Hanover case is contemporaneous court reporting.
- Stoll's book and oral histories are central to the human story, but they should be treated as participant accounts.
- Avoid turning the 75-cent discrepancy into a magic clue. It mattered because Stoll cared enough to investigate a small accounting anomaly and then built evidence over time.
- Avoid giving intrusion procedure. Focus on logging, evidence, institutional response, and historical infrastructure.
- The public record found so far is strong enough for historical grounding; German court files would only be needed for a legal monograph-level account.
