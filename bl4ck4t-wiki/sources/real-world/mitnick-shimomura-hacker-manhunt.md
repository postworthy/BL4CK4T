## Claims To Verify In Historical Account

- Mitnick was arrested in Raleigh, North Carolina, in February 1995.
- Shimomura's systems were intruded upon in late 1994, and he participated in technical tracing work that helped locate Mitnick.
- Prosecutors framed Mitnick's conduct through computer fraud, wire fraud, possession of unauthorized access devices, and related charges.
- Mitnick spent years in custody before final sentencing and supervised-release conditions.
- The Free Kevin movement and later public reputation shift are central to the cultural history.
- Media accounts often amplified fear of invisible technical power.
