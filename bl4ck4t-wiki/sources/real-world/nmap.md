# Nmap

## Summary

Nmap is a documented open-source tool for network exploration and security auditing. In BL4CK4T, it should only appear in authorized labs or fictional systems.

## Defensive Lesson

- Defenders need to know what systems and services they own.
- Discovery belongs in authorized environments.
- Scan results require interpretation and verification.

## Unsafe Details To Avoid Publicly

- Do not encourage scanning networks without permission.
- Avoid advanced stealth/evasion options in public youth-facing stories.

## BL4CK4T Transformation Notes

- Fictional object: Byte's city map lantern that reveals classroom-lab doors and service signs.
- Location fit: [The Old Stack](../../locations/cybertropolis.md#the-old-stack).
- Lesson fit: inventory, authorization, and service awareness.
