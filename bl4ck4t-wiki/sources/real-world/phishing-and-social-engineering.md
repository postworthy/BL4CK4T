# Phishing And Social Engineering

## Summary

CISA describes phishing as suspicious messages designed to trick people into taking unsafe actions. The defensive lesson is to slow down, verify the sender, avoid suspicious links or attachments, use strong account protections, and report suspicious messages.

## Defensive Lesson

- Urgency, fear, prizes, and unexpected requests can be warning signs.
- Sender identity, link destination, and message context should be checked.
- Password managers and strong passwords reduce account takeover risk.
- Reporting suspicious messages helps protect others.

## Unsafe Details To Avoid Publicly

- Do not provide templates for phishing messages.
- Do not explain how to bypass mail filtering.
- Do not provide credential-harvesting mechanics.
