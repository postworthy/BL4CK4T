# ILOVEYOU Worm

## Summary

ILOVEYOU is a historical email worm remembered for spreading through trust, curiosity, and unsafe attachment handling. The BL4CK4T lesson is that appealing messages can hide harmful actions.

## Defensive Lesson

- Unexpected attachments need caution.
- Social trust can be abused.
- File names and appearances can mislead.
- Safe defaults and user education matter.

## Unsafe Details To Avoid Publicly

- Do not provide script behavior or replication steps.
- Do not recreate attachment tricks.
