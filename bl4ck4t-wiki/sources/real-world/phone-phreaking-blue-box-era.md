# Phone Phreaking And The Blue-Box Era

## Purpose

Source page for the real-world historical material behind Season 1: phone phreaking, blue boxes, in-band telephone signaling, the Bell System, early phreak culture, the Captain Crunch whistle story, and the Silicon Valley mythology around Steve Wozniak and Steve Jobs.

## Extraction Notes

- The strongest technical anchor is the in-band signaling design: user-audible paths and network control signals were not fully separated in older long-distance telephone systems.
- Rosenbaum's 1971 Esquire article is the strongest public-culture inflection point.
- Treat the Captain Crunch whistle story as myth-heavy. The current research foundation credits Sid Bernay ahead of John Draper for the whistle discovery while recognizing Draper's public role.
- Treat Jobs/Wozniak material as a historically supported origin myth about experimentation, confidence, and product-building, not as proof that phone phreaking mechanically caused Apple.
- Do not use operational construction details from period phreak documents in public-facing fiction. Use architectural ideas, cultural context, consequences, and ethical tension.
- The historical foundation is strong enough for season-level story anchoring; court records would only be needed for a legal-detail article focused on specific prosecutions.
