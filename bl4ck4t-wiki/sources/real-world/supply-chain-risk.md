# Supply Chain Risk

## Summary

Cyber supply-chain risk comes from dependence on outside software, services, vendors, packages, and components. The BL4CK4T lesson is to check provenance, reduce blind trust, and verify what enters a system.

## Defensive Lesson

- Know what components are in use.
- Prefer trusted sources and signed releases when available.
- Review dependencies and permissions.
- Monitor for vendor or package compromise signals.

## Unsafe Details To Avoid Publicly

- Do not describe package-poisoning steps.
- Do not provide typosquatting or maintainer-abuse tactics.
