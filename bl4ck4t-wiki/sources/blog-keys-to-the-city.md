# Keys To The City: Public Private Crypto

## Summary

This article follows a BL4CK4T drop about two strangers creating a secret in the open. The Script Kitties learn public/private cryptography, digital signatures, and Diffie-Hellman-style shared secrets.

## Canon Contributions

- BL4CK4T drops can appear as clues on rooftop billboards.
- Rooftops are a recurring learning/staging location.
- Grimalkin recognizes patterns and names likely concepts.
- Cipher explains public/private cryptography.
- Pixel uses analogies and wants to test Diffie-Hellman.
- Byte builds a toy RSA demo.

## Lesson Contributions

- Public/private cryptography uses different keys for encryption/decryption or signing/verification.
- The mailbox analogy explains public encryption and private decryption.
- Diffie-Hellman can be explained through shared color mixing.
- HTTPS, digital signatures, and secure login rely on asymmetric cryptography and trust.
