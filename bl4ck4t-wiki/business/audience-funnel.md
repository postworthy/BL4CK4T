# Audience Funnel

## Summary

Every BL4CK4T audience should have a clear route from curiosity into deeper participation. The Field Guide is the first shared conversion point, while later CTAs should branch toward products, classroom resources, or BL4CK4T University.

## Audience Routes

| Audience | First hook | First conversion | Later conversion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Young readers | Characters, mystery, art, quotes | Start with Season 1 | Stickers, posters, decks |
| Parents | Safe curiosity and digital wisdom | Field Guide | Family case files, notebooks |
| Homeschoolers | Story-based STEM unit | Classroom starter pack | Cyber Club curriculum |
| Teachers/librarians | Ready discussion material | Printable prompt | Case Night kit |
| Cyber professionals | Clever historical translation | Behind the Signal | Amplification, advising |
| Enterprise buyers | Memorable security culture | University pilot page | Training modules, tabletop |
| Sponsors/partners | Mission-aligned education | Partnership inquiry | Sponsored kits, campaigns |

## Primary Lead Magnet

`The BL4CK4T Field Guide: 7 Cyber Habits for Young Defenders`

Core habits:

1. Know what you protect - know what matters, who owns it, and who could be hurt.
2. Guard the trusted paths - keep doors, keys, updates, and message paths trustworthy.
3. Watch for strange signals - notice logs, timing, silence, repeats, and mismatches.
4. Keep evidence before story - record facts, theories, ruled-out ideas, and unknowns.
5. Report early, kindly, and clearly - fast reporting protects the next person.
6. Recover with consent and care - restore known-good paths with verification and permission.
7. Improve the city after the case - turn each case into a better habit, checklist, map, or warning.

## Site CTA Map

- Home: Start Here and Field Guide.
- Season pages: Read episode one, download Field Guide, or save the season as a classroom case.
- Blog posts: next episode, Field Guide, and one relevant artifact/product CTA.
- Store: Shopify product links or waitlists.
- Classroom: case files, cyber club starter kit, printable prompts.
- University: pilot request and enterprise module previews.
- Newsletter: weekly field notes, product drops, classroom ideas, and behind-the-signal notes.
