# Metrics Dashboard Specification

## Summary

BL4CK4T should measure trust, engagement, conversion, and product demand rather than treating likes as the main success signal.

## Awareness Metrics

- Impressions.
- Reach.
- Follower growth.
- Profile visits.
- Video views.
- Watch completion.
- Search impressions.

## Engagement Metrics

- Saves.
- Shares.
- Comments.
- Replies.
- Poll participation.
- Carousel completion.
- Rewatch rate.
- Thread engagement.
- Newsletter replies.

## Conversion Metrics

- Blog visits.
- Season page visits.
- Field Guide downloads.
- Newsletter signups.
- Store visits.
- Product waitlist signups.
- Digital download purchases.
- Classroom kit inquiries.
- BL4CK4T University inquiries.
- Partner or sponsor inquiries.

## Influence Metrics

- Security professionals engaging.
- Teachers requesting resources.
- Parents sharing posts.
- Librarians or homeschool leaders asking for kits.
- Conferences, podcasts, or newsletters reaching out.
- Repeat commenters across platforms.
- Organic mentions.

## Highest-Value Signals

Prioritize:

- saves;
- shares;
- email signups;
- teacher, parent, and librarian comments;
- security professional comments;
- product waitlist signups;
- partnership inquiries;
- repeat engagement.

## UTM Defaults

Use consistent UTM parameters:

- `utm_source`: platform name.
- `utm_medium`: social, email, paid, partner, organic.
- `utm_campaign`: season or principle campaign.
- `utm_content`: content type, such as trailer, carousel, quote-card, artifact-card, thread, pin.
