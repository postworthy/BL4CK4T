# Product Roadmap

## Summary

BL4CK4T products should feel like artifacts from Cybertropolis. The product line should validate demand with simple digital and physical products before expanding into curriculum, case decks, enterprise modules, licensing, or a custom platform.

## Phase 1: Foundation

- Keep the blog ad-free.
- Add public `Start Here`, `Field Guide`, `Store`, `Classroom`, `University`, and `Newsletter` pages.
- Launch the newsletter and Field Guide lead magnet.
- Add store waitlists for posters, stickers, character cards, and field notebooks.
- Create the first digital case file concept for Season 5.
- Create BL4CK4T University pilot interest capture.

## Phase 2: Product Validation

- Launch `The Love Letter Plague` digital case file.
- Launch poster and sticker products through Shopify.
- Launch the Script Kitties character card pack.
- Open preorders or interest capture for `The Love Letter Plague` Case Deck.
- Collect feedback from parents, teachers, cyber clubs, and security professionals.

## Phase 3: Education Expansion

- Build `BL4CK4T Cyber Club: Semester One` around Seasons 1-3.
- Create library and school event kits.
- Add facilitator guides, printable case boards, and student reflection prompts.
- Package `Case Night` as a 60-90 minute activity.

## Phase 4: Enterprise Pilot

- Build `Report Without Shame` from Season 5.
- Build `Silence Is Not A Fourth Option` from Season 7.
- Build `Availability Is Trust` from Season 6.
- Offer tabletop facilitation to small teams before building LMS modules.

## Phase 5: Scaled Ecosystem

- Publish season books and collected editions.
- Release full case deck collection.
- Launch membership only if it deepens the world without paywalling main canon.
- Explore school, library, conference, and enterprise licensing.
- Build a digital platform only after content and buyer demand are proven.

9. Homeschool Cyber Club Starter Kit.
10. BL4CK4T University: `Report Without Shame`.
