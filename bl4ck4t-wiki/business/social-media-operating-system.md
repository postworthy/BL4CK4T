# Social Media Operating System

## Summary

Social media is the signal layer for BL4CK4T. The goal is not simply to promote posts; it is to make audiences meet the characters, remember the principles, feel Cybertropolis, and choose to go deeper through the blog, Field Guide, newsletter, products, classroom resources, or BL4CK4T University.

## Universal Funnel

1. Encounter the world.
2. Recognize a character, artifact, or phrase.
3. Engage with a short piece of content.
4. Visit the blog, season page, or start-here page.
5. Download the Field Guide.
6. Join the newsletter.
7. Buy a product, use a classroom resource, or request a pilot.
8. Become a fan, educator, sponsor, partner, or customer.

## Platform Roles

- Instagram: comic gallery for posters, character cards, quote cards, carousel scenes, and artifact cards.
- TikTok/Reels: short-form story engine for micro-trailers, POV scenes, choose-the-next-move prompts, and principle shorts.
- YouTube Shorts: evergreen searchable archive for concept explainers, season trailers, character intros, and artifact explainers.
- X: real-time cyber commentary for security professionals, educators, journalists, founders, and conference organizers.
- LinkedIn: enterprise credibility channel for security awareness, compliance training, culture, and pilot partnerships.
- Facebook: parent, homeschool, teacher, librarian, and local community channel.
- Pinterest: evergreen visual discovery for posters, printables, classroom activities, and artifact pins.
- Reddit: credibility and feedback channel; participate carefully, disclose affiliation, and avoid sales language.
- Email: owned audience for story updates, product launches, classroom resources, and partner interest.

## Campaign Priorities

First 90-day content focus:

1. Season 5: broadest family, classroom, and enterprise fit.
2. Season 7: strongest enterprise and IT/security-leader fit.
3. Season 1: best world-entry and visual identity fit.
4. Season 2: strongest cyber-professional credibility fit.
5. Season 6: strongest leadership, resilience, and operations fit.

## Episode Derivative Workflow

For every episode, extract:

- core scene;
- principle;
- primary character;
- artifact;
- audience fit;
- CTA destination;
- quote card;
- artifact or principle card;
- short video script;
- classroom or parent prompt;
- enterprise lesson when relevant;
- product or case-deck idea.

## Field Guide Habits

- Know what you protect.
- Guard the trusted paths.
- Watch for strange signals.
- Keep evidence before story.
- Report early, kindly, and clearly.
- Recover with consent and care.
- Improve the city after the case.

## Recurring Principles

- Evidence before story.
- Small does not mean empty.
- Curious. Contained.
- Intent starts the story. Impact finishes it.
- You are not in trouble for trusting someone.
- We do not know yet.
- Availability is trust.
- Silence is not a fourth option.
- Helpful repair without consent is still risk.
- Leave the city better than you found it.

## CTA Rules

Use native, low-friction CTAs:

- Read the full case.
- Enter Cybertropolis.
- Start with Season 1.
- Download the free Field Guide.
- Save this for your cyber club.
- Teachers: use this as a discussion prompt.
- Request the BL4CK4T University pilot.

Avoid CTAs that feel like sales interruptions inside story-first content.
