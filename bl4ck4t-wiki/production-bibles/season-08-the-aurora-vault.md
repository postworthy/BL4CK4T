# Season 8: The Aurora Vault Production Bible

## Production Purpose

Guide the complete Season 8 release, keeping the story historically anchored, emotionally clear, and publishable as a simultaneous nine-episode drop.

## Release Strategy

Publish all nine public posts and the public season page together after draft, continuity, historical-anchor, entity, trope, wiki, and build validation pass.

## Source Chain

Real-world source page -> historical account -> documentary treatment -> story-world import -> support canon -> production bible -> mission packets -> drafts -> public posts.

## Season Promise

A copy can be dangerous when it reveals how tomorrow is protected. The public story should keep the scale human and make crown-jewel protection, access review, and quiet anomaly reading concrete.

## Character Control

- Lead: Jinx.
- Secondary: Grimalkin.
- Growth line: Some thieves do not steal the jewel. They copy the map to the jewel.

## Canon Controls

- Artifact: Crown Jewel Register.
- Location: Aurora Vault.
- Faction: Aurora Caretakers.
- Villain: The Glass Dragon.
- Cliffhanger: A copied blueprint points beneath the city toward an old physical machine.

## Publication Voice

Narrative-first. Keep jokes and warmth, but let consequence breathe. Avoid lecturing the reader or adding explicit safety-parenting language inside public story prose.

## Validation Gate

Complete draft review, continuity audit, entity inventory, historical-anchor analysis, pnpm tropes:check, pnpm wiki:check, and pnpm build before commit.
