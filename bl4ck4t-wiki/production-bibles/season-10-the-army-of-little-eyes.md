# Season 10: The Army Of Little Eyes Production Bible

## Production Purpose

Guide the complete Season 10 release, keeping the story historically anchored, emotionally clear, and publishable as a simultaneous nine-episode drop.

## Release Strategy

Publish all nine public posts and the public season page together after draft, continuity, historical-anchor, entity, trope, wiki, and build validation pass.

## Source Chain

Real-world source page -> historical account -> documentary treatment -> story-world import -> support canon -> production bible -> mission packets -> drafts -> public posts.

## Season Promise

Forgotten devices can still answer someone else. The public story should keep the scale human and make inventory, safer defaults, education, and manufacturer responsibility concrete.

## Character Control

- Lead: Pixel.
- Secondary: Jinx.
- Growth line: Forgotten is not the same as free.

## Canon Controls

- Artifact: Device Census.
- Location: Little Things District.
- Faction: Nameplate Factory.
- Villain: The Shepherd Of Little Eyes.
- Cliffhanger: The repeated names trace back to a shared update supplier.

## Episode Grid

| Episode | Title | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Toy Owl Blinks | Pixel sees a toy answer a call nobody in the room made. |
| 2 | Ordinary Doors | Traffic comes through homes, classrooms, shops, and closets. |
| 3 | Please Do Not Blame Them | Whiskers stops the city from shaming device owners. |
| 4 | The Device Census | Jinx begins counting every connected thing that can answer. |
| 5 | Factory Names | The team finds repeated names and default keys across many devices. |
| 6 | Nameplate Factory | Byte asks why so many devices shipped with the same weak habits. |
| 7 | The Shepherd Calls | The Shepherd gathers devices he does not own. |
| 8 | Caretaker Checklist | Pixel teaches owners to rename, update, isolate, or retire devices. |
| 9 | Better Names | A toy gets a safer name, and the final clue points at the maker. |

## Publication Voice

Narrative-first. Keep jokes and warmth, but let consequence breathe. Avoid lecturing the reader or adding explicit safety-parenting language inside public story prose.

## Validation Gate

Complete draft review, continuity audit, entity inventory, historical-anchor analysis, pnpm tropes:check, pnpm wiki:check, and pnpm build before commit.
