## Season Promise

Season 7 promises a maintenance story. Cybertropolis is not fooled by a new message or flooded by repeated requests. It is forced to face an old known City Runtime repair that looked easy to postpone because the visible systems still worked. The season teaches that repair must be timely, verified, reversible where possible, and authorized.

## Story Engine

The engine is the tension between delay and forced repair. The Red Clerk is wrong because he delays a known runtime repair to avoid disruption. The Helpful Ghost is wrong because it repairs without consent. The Script Kitties must build a third path: trusted, accountable, visible repair.

## Tone

Use:

- public signs that look normal until they suddenly do not;
- desk terminals, service-room machines, classrooms, clinic intake stations, permit counters, weather panels, library checkout stands, and public boards;
- maintenance rooms, repair cards, rollback shelves, and verification lamps;
- Cipher's fear of making a wrong call;
- Byte's fascination with a clever fix before he sees its missing consent;
- ordinary people affected by rebooting desks and stale public pages.

Avoid:

- real worm mechanics, exploit paths, payload details, or scanning behavior;
- making the Helpful Ghost heroic;
- making the Red Clerk a simple villain without his fear of past broken repairs;
- repeating Season 3's escaped-experiment emotional shape;
- treating patching as boring compliance instead of civic care.

## Character Production Notes

- Cipher carries responsible action under bounded evidence. Her season wound is fear of making a wrong call.
- Byte carries consent-aware engineering. His wound is admiration for clever repair that works.
- Jinx verifies source, signature, and ledger trail.
- Grimalkin forces rollback and risk acceptance into the process.
- Whiskers explains public maintenance as civic care.
- Pixel makes patch debt tangible for younger readers.
- Shadow spots signs that look repaired but still behave wrong.
- BL4CK4T asks questions; he does not solve the repair.
