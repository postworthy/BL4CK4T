# Season 7: The Patch Bell War Production Bible

## Production Purpose

Guide the complete Season 7 release, keeping the story historically anchored, emotionally clear, and publishable as a simultaneous nine-episode drop.

## Release Strategy

Publish all nine public posts and the public season page together after draft, continuity, historical-anchor, entity, trope, wiki, and build validation pass.

## Source Chain

Real-world source page -> historical account -> documentary treatment -> story-world import -> support canon -> production bible -> mission packets -> drafts -> public posts.

## Season Promise

A known flaw ignored long enough becomes an emergency everyone inherits. The public story should keep the scale human and make patch, isolate, rollback, and consent-aware emergency repair concrete.

## Character Control

- Lead: Cipher.
- Secondary: Byte.
- Growth line: A fix that enters without asking still breaks the door.

## Canon Controls

- Artifact: Patch Bell.
- Location: Civic Pages District.
- Faction: Repair Lane.
- Villain: The Red Clerk.
- Cliffhanger: A quiet log shows source plans were read before the loud repair crisis.

## Publication Voice

Narrative-first. Keep jokes and warmth, but let consequence breathe. Avoid lecturing the reader or adding explicit safety-parenting language inside public story prose.
