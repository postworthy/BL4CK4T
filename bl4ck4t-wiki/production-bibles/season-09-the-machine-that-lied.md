# Season 9: The Machine That Lied Production Bible

## Production Purpose

Guide the complete Season 9 release, keeping the story historically anchored, emotionally clear, and publishable as a simultaneous nine-episode drop.

## Release Strategy

Publish all nine public posts and the public season page together after draft, continuity, historical-anchor, entity, trope, wiki, and build validation pass.

## Source Chain

Real-world source page -> historical account -> documentary treatment -> story-world import -> support canon -> production bible -> mission packets -> drafts -> public posts.

## Season Promise

A screen is only one witness when machines can affect the physical world. The public story should keep the scale human and make independent sensing, manual checks, and safety-first response concrete.

## Character Control

- Lead: Shadow.
- Secondary: Byte.
- Growth line: The screen said normal. The floor said run.

## Canon Controls

- Artifact: Truth Gauge.
- Location: Foundry Quarter.
- Faction: Foundry Caretakers.
- Villain: The False Engineer.
- Cliffhanger: Pixel notices tiny devices answering under repeated factory names.

## Publication Voice

Narrative-first. Keep jokes and warmth, but let consequence breathe. Avoid lecturing the reader or adding explicit safety-parenting language inside public story prose.

## Validation Gate

Complete draft review, continuity audit, entity inventory, historical-anchor analysis, pnpm tropes:check, pnpm wiki:check, and pnpm build before commit.
