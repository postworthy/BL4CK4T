# Season 6: The Day The City Would Not Answer Production Bible

## Production Purpose

Guide the complete Season 6 release, keeping the story historically anchored, emotionally clear, and publishable as a simultaneous nine-episode drop.

## Release Strategy

Publish all nine public posts and the public season page together after draft, continuity, historical-anchor, entity, trope, wiki, and build validation pass.

## Source Chain

Real-world source page -> historical account -> documentary treatment -> story-world import -> support canon -> production bible -> mission packets -> drafts -> public posts.

## Season Promise

A service that cannot answer can still harm people who depend on it. The public story should keep the scale human and make honest status, triage, and resilient service design concrete.

## Character Control

- Lead: Whiskers.
- Secondary: Jinx.
- Growth line: A leader who guesses loudly can make a quiet crisis worse.

## Canon Controls

- Artifact: Service Map.
- Location: Queue District.
- Faction: Status Wall Desk.
- Villain: The Flood Prince.
- Cliffhanger: One red service did not fail because of traffic. It failed because it had been left unpatched.

## Episode Grid

| Episode | Title | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Spinning Board | Clinic forms spin, lunch lines freeze, and Whiskers learns that silence can be a crisis. |
| 2 | Nothing Missing | The team checks records and finds no theft, only unanswered services. |
| 3 | The Service Map | Jinx maps delayed, degraded, and critical systems while people wait. |
| 4 | We Do Not Know Yet | Whiskers writes the first honest status note and feels the city watching. |
| 5 | Many Knocks | Pixel sees the repeated question that keeps every desk occupied. |
| 6 | Who Answers First | The team chooses priority lanes for clinics, schools, and help boards. |
| 7 | The Flood Crown | The Flood Prince claims a blocked door is harmless because nothing was stolen. |
| 8 | Waiting Room Weather | A witness episode shows why delay becomes harm for ordinary citizens. |
| 9 | The Red Service | The city recovers, then the Service Map shows one failure with an older cause. |

## Publication Voice

Narrative-first. Keep jokes and warmth, but let consequence breathe. Avoid lecturing the reader or adding explicit safety-parenting language inside public story prose.

## Validation Gate

Complete draft review, continuity audit, entity inventory, historical-anchor analysis, pnpm tropes:check, pnpm wiki:check, and pnpm build before commit.
