# Account Misuse

## Summary

Account misuse happens when an account, pass, or identity is used in a way the rightful owner did not intend. In stories, this is represented through the Borrowed Door: a system sees a familiar pass, but the use does not fit the person or context.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Accounts represent trust.
- Unusual timing, location, or resource use can matter.
- Account owners may be victims, not culprits.
- Investigators should avoid accusations until evidence is clear.
- Recovery includes protecting the owner and the system.

## Season 2 Use

The Borrowed Door lets the season discuss unauthorized account use from the defender's view without showing how access was obtained or abused.

## Story Hooks

- A project pass appearing in a room its owner never entered.
- A resource charge made while the real user was at a club meeting.
- Whiskers stopping the team from blaming the account owner too early.
