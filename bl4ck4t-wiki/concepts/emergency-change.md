# Emergency Change

## Summary

Emergency Change is a Season 7 teaching concept tied to MSBlaster and Welchia/Nachi. It supports the season lesson: A known flaw ignored long enough becomes an emergency everyone inherits.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

In story terms, this concept helps the Script Kitties turn a confusing civic incident into a visible habit: patch, isolate, rollback, and consent-aware emergency repair.

## Story Hooks

- Use when the team needs to explain the season's main risk without showing harmful procedure.
- Connect the idea to Patch Bell and the growth arc for Cipher.
- Keep the lesson practical, kind, and grounded in defender behavior.
