# Emergency Change

## Summary

Emergency change is a fast defensive change made under pressure when waiting for normal process would create greater harm.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Emergency action should still be bounded, logged, reversible where possible, and communicated.
- Acting with incomplete evidence is different from acting carelessly.
- Cipher uses this concept to learn responsible action under pressure.

## Story Hooks

- A repair card with a short clock.
- A rollback plan written before the fix.
- The Consent Ledger showing who authorized the change.
