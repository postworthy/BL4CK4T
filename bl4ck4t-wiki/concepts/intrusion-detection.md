# Intrusion Detection

## Summary

Intrusion detection is the practice of noticing behavior that may indicate unauthorized access, misuse, or unexpected system activity. It is defender work: watching, comparing, preserving, and escalating evidence.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Detection starts with knowing normal.
- An alert is a question, not a verdict.
- Evidence should be preserved before conclusions harden.
- Detection tools should help people investigate, not replace judgment.
- Defenders coordinate with system owners instead of chasing blindly.

## Season 2 Use

The Ledger Lab does not know it has a serious problem at first. The team gradually builds detection around resource mismatches, timing, terminal behavior, and repeated patterns.

## Story Hooks

- A monitor that only lights when two records disagree.
- Shadow noticing that a terminal is warm after hours.
- Whiskers asking what evidence would make the Glass Bureau listen.
