# Civic Resilience

## Summary

Civic resilience is the ability of a city, school, service, or community to keep essential work moving during disruption and recover with better habits afterward.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Resilience is preparation plus response plus learning.
- Fallback paths matter before the crisis.
- People, procedures, and communication are part of the system.
- A city can become stronger without pretending nothing went wrong.

## Story Hooks

- Grimalkin sets priority lanes for services people need most.
- The Status Wall becomes a public habit after the flood.
- The city keeps some local services answering while outside gates are noisy.
