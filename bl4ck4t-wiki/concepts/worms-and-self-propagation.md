# Worms And Self-Propagation

## Summary

A worm is software that can spread without attaching to another program. In BL4CK4T stories, this becomes fictional copying behavior that teaches propagation risk without explaining real abuse paths.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

Use fictional copying markers, rooms, resource counters, process slots, and trust paths to teach spread and resource pressure.

## Story Hooks

- The Copycat Sprite is a story metaphor for self-propagation.
- The core lesson is spread plus resource pressure, not file destruction.
- The audience should understand why connected systems magnify mistakes.
- Old trust paths can let a copying process reach systems outside the builder's intended boundary.

## Public Boundary

Do not explain real replication methods. Show copying through counters, process slots, rooms, queues, trust-path maps, and containment maps.
