# Ransomware

## Summary

Ransomware is malicious software that locks or encrypts data and demands payment. BL4CK4T stories should focus on prevention, backups, segmentation, and recovery planning.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Keep backups protected.
- Patch known weaknesses.
- Limit how far trouble can spread.
- Practice recovery.

## Story Hooks

- Locked library shelves.
- Frozen neon files.
- Backup vaults under The Old Stack.
