# Supply Chain Trust

## Summary

Supply-chain trust is the practice of understanding where components come from, how they change, and whether they should be trusted.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Check the source.
- Verify seals and signatures.
- Review permissions.
- Keep an inventory.
- Prefer trusted, maintained components.

## Story Hooks

- Packet Market crates.
- Forged seals.
- Tool carts with mismatched labels.
