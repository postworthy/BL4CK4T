# Patching

## Summary

Patching means applying updates that fix known weaknesses. In BL4CK4T, patching is maintenance that closes old doors before trouble finds them.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Know what you own.
- Update in a timely way.
- Test and verify important fixes.
- Prioritize exposed and known-exploited weaknesses.

## Story Hooks

- Flickering lock plates.
- Old tunnels in The Old Stack.
- Patch lanterns that seal cracks.
