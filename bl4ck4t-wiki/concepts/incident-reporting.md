# Incident Reporting

## Summary

Incident reporting turns a finding into a message that the right caretakers can understand and act on. A report needs facts, scope, uncertainty, and next steps.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Report what you know and how you know it.
- Separate facts from guesses.
- Include scope and affected systems.
- Name what remains unknown.
- Send the report to the responsible caretaker or institution.

## Season 2 Use

The Glass Bureau cannot act on Jinx's suspicion alone. The team has to turn the Threadboard into a report that crosses school, library, museum, and research relay boundaries.

## Story Hooks

- Whiskers rewriting a dramatic accusation into a useful report.
- Jinx learning to say unknown without feeling defeated.
- A Glass Bureau clerk finally understanding the pattern because the report is clear.
