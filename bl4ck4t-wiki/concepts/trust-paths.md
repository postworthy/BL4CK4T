# Trust Paths

## Summary

Trust paths are old approved connections that let one Cybertropolis system reach another because they once needed to share tools, classrooms, or project resources.

In Season 3, old trust paths explain why the Copycat Sprite can reach The Old Stack even though Byte's drawn sandbox did not name those rooms.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

Trust paths teach that connected systems inherit assumptions from earlier designs. A path that was convenient for trusted classrooms can become dangerous when a copying process reaches it.

## Story Hooks

- The Old Stack still has trust paths from old Ledger Lab learning rooms.
- Jinx and Cipher separate confirmed trust paths from guessed paths on the Copy Map.
- Grimalkin's containment lanes close or guard trust paths until responders understand the spread.

## Public Boundary

Do not describe real remote trust exploitation. Keep the focus on inherited connections, defender mapping, isolation, and review.

## Canon Notes

- Trust paths are broader than Season 3, but Season 3 is where they become a direct explanation for propagation.
- Future seasons may reuse the concept for old integrations, inherited access, or forgotten system dependencies.
