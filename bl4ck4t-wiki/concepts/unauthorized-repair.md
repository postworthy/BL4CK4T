# Unauthorized Repair

## Summary

Unauthorized repair is a change made to someone else's system without permission, even when the change appears to fix a real problem.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Good intent does not create permission.
- A repair that breaks trust can create a second incident.
- Defenders should build trusted channels instead of admiring forced fixes.

## Story Hooks

- A fixed door with no signature.
- A silver pawprint where a caretaker stamp should be.
- Byte saying `it worked` before realizing what was missing.
