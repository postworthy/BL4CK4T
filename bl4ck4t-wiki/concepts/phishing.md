# Phishing

## Summary

Phishing is trickery through messages that try to make someone click, open, reply, pay, or reveal something sensitive.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Slow down.
- Check sender and context.
- Inspect links without clicking.
- Ask a trusted adult or teammate.
- Report suspicious messages.

## Story Hooks

- Fake city alerts.
- Prize invitations.
- Almost-correct badges.
- Message signs that shimmer wrong when inspected.
