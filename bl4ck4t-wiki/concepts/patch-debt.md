# Patch Debt

## Summary

Patch debt is the accumulated risk from known fixes that have not yet been applied, isolated, or formally risk-accepted.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- A known repair notice changes the moral state of delay.
- Delay may be necessary, but it should be visible, time-bounded, and owned.
- If a system cannot be patched immediately, it needs isolation, monitoring, rollback planning, or public risk acceptance.

## Story Hooks

- Red repair cards under normal-looking signs.
- Old cracks with fresh warning tags.
- The Patch Bell ringing before the fever appears.
