# Verified Updates

## Summary

Verified updates are repairs delivered through a trusted source, checked for authenticity, authorized by the right caretaker, and paired with rollback or recovery planning.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Urgency does not remove the need to verify the source.
- A real repair should have a real path.
- Citizens need clear communication so false repair notices cannot hide beside true ones.

## Story Hooks

- Repair Lane lamps.
- Signed update cards.
- Jinx checking whether a notice belongs to the trusted path.
