# Trace Evidence

## Summary

Trace evidence is partial evidence left by movement through a system: times, paths, handoffs, repeated marks, mismatched records, and clues that suggest a route without proving every step.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- A trace is a clue, not a full story.
- Preserve timing and order.
- Compare traces against alternative explanations.
- Avoid naming a culprit before the evidence supports it.

## Story Hooks

- Shadow notices that a route badge is warm when the room is empty.
- Jinx builds a chase map that labels confirmed, likely, ruled out, and unknown paths.
- Cipher removes a pattern that looks dramatic but does not match the records.
