# Public Mythology

## Summary

Public mythology is the story a crowd starts repeating before all the evidence is known. In Season 4, it appears through headlines, hallway rumors, posters, and names that become larger than the facts.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Separate what was seen from what was guessed.
- Ask who benefits from the simplest version of a story.
- Notice when fear turns a person into a symbol.
- Preserve harm and accountability without exaggeration.

## Story Hooks

- A notice wall fills with claims faster than the team can verify them.
- The City Chronicle chooses a dramatic nickname before Jinx has a timeline.
- Shadow learns that being unseen can protect people or distort trust.
