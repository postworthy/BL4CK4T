# Status Communication

## Summary

Status Communication is a Season 6 teaching concept tied to Estonia 2007 cyberattacks. It supports the season lesson: A service that cannot answer can still harm people who depend on it.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

In story terms, this concept helps the Script Kitties turn a confusing civic incident into a visible habit: honest status, triage, and resilient service design.

## Story Hooks

- Use when the team needs to explain the season's main risk without showing harmful procedure.
- Connect the idea to Service Map and the growth arc for Whiskers.
- Keep the lesson practical, kind, and grounded in defender behavior.
