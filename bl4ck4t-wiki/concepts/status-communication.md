# Status Communication

## Summary

Status communication is the practice of telling people what is working, degraded, unavailable, unknown, and changing during an incident.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Honest uncertainty is better than loud guessing.
- People need useful next steps, not blame.
- Status should separate facts, estimates, and unknowns.
- Updates should change when evidence changes.

## Story Hooks

- Whiskers writes `we do not know yet` on the Status Wall.
- Jinx adds timestamps to status changes.
- Cipher marks a claim as unproven even when the city wants an answer.
