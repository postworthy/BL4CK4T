# Network Discovery

## Summary

Network discovery maps systems and services in an authorized environment. In BL4CK4T, it is a defender's inventory skill, not permission to poke unknown networks.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Ask permission first.
- Use lab networks.
- Record what you find.
- Verify results.
- Fix exposure when appropriate.

## Story Hooks

- Byte's map lantern.
- Service signs on classroom-lab doors.
- The Old Stack inventory walk.
