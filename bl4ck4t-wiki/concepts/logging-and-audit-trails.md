# Logging And Audit Trails

## Summary

Logging records what systems did, when they did it, and which accounts or components were involved. Audit trails turn those records into an ordered path that can be reviewed later.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Logs are records, not guesses.
- Good records need time, context, and preservation.
- A single log line rarely proves the whole story.
- Audit trails help defenders explain what changed and when.
- Logs should protect privacy while preserving useful evidence.

## Season 2 Use

The Threadboard uses logs and audit trails to turn Jinx's `-0.75` hunch into a case other people can understand.
