# Device Inventory

## Summary

Device Inventory is a Season 10 teaching concept tied to Mirai and the 2016 Dyn DDoS attack. It supports the season lesson: Forgotten devices can still answer someone else.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

In story terms, this concept helps the Script Kitties turn a confusing civic incident into a visible habit: inventory, safer defaults, education, and manufacturer responsibility.

## Story Hooks

- Use when the team needs to explain the season's main risk without showing harmful procedure.
- Connect the idea to Device Census and the growth arc for Pixel.
- Keep the lesson practical, kind, and grounded in defender behavior.
