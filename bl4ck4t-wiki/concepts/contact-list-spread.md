# Contact List Spread

## Summary

Contact list spread is the way a message can travel through trusted relationships after one person opens or forwards it. The trust between people becomes the path.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Trusted contacts can send unsafe messages by accident.
- Stop spread by reporting and pausing, not by blaming friends.
- Warnings should explain what to do next.
- Clean-up includes people, not only systems.

## Story Hooks

- A message from a friend arrives at the wrong hour.
- The Glitter Letter blooms through class lists.
- Jinx helps the city warn contacts without turning victims into suspects.
