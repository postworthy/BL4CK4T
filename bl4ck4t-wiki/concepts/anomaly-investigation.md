# Anomaly Investigation

## Summary

Anomaly investigation is the work of understanding something that does not fit expected behavior. The anomaly may be harmless, mistaken, or important. The investigator's job is to find out which.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Small does not mean empty.
- Strange does not mean guilty.
- Learn normal before naming abnormal.
- Keep multiple explanations alive until evidence narrows them.
- Document what changed your mind.

## Season 2 Use

Jinx begins with a small mismatch. Her arc depends on learning to prove why it matters instead of treating her first suspicion as enough.

## Story Hooks

- Jinx building a normal-noise list for the Ledger Lab.
- Grimalkin pinning alternate explanations beside the main theory.
- Pixel discovering that boring records can be exciting when they disagree.
