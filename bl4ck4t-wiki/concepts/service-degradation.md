# Service Degradation

## Summary

Service degradation means a service is still partly working but slower, limited, unreliable, or only available to some users.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Outages are not always all-or-nothing.
- `Working`, `degraded`, `unavailable`, and `unknown` are different states.
- Honest status helps people choose fallback paths.
- Degraded service may still need urgent care if people depend on it.

## Story Hooks

- A school board answers every third request.
- A public kiosk works inside the city but not from outside gates.
- Whiskers corrects a status note from `fine` to `degraded`.
