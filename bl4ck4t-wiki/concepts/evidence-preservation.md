# Evidence Preservation

## Summary

Evidence preservation keeps records intact so they can be reviewed, explained, and trusted later. A clue that is changed, lost, or stripped of context may no longer support a reliable conclusion.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Preserve before editing.
- Keep timestamps and context.
- Record who saw what and when.
- Avoid changing the scene more than necessary.
- Make evidence clear enough for someone outside the team to follow.

## Season 2 Use

Byte's Threadboard preserves the order of clues. Whiskers pushes the team to keep evidence fair enough for caretakers and the Glass Bureau to act on.

## Story Hooks

- A print slip that matters because Shadow saves it before the tray is cleared.
- Byte making the Threadboard show original records beside notes.
- Jinx learning that a good case survives someone else's review.
