# Service Rooms

## Summary

Service rooms are the back-office machine rooms that help Cybertropolis desk terminals, public boards, forms, printers, queues, and counters talk to one another.

They are less visible than public signs or desk terminals, but failures inside them can make ordinary systems slow, unstable, or unreachable.

## Story Function

- Give Season 7 a clear place for backend systems, support queues, update staging, and emergency maintenance.
- Support worm-era lessons about exposed services, inventory, isolation, and patch deployment.
- Let defenders act through service maps, logs, update status, and rollback checks instead of vague metaphor.

## Visual Motifs

- Racks of humming service boxes.
- Status lamps, update shelves, cable labels, and handwritten maintenance windows.
- Caretaker benches with repair cards, rollback notes, and inventory sheets.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

Service rooms teach that users often experience failures at the desk, but defenders must also understand the systems behind the desk. Good response requires knowing which services are exposed, which machines are patched, which can be isolated, and which must keep running.

## Story Hooks

- A service-room log reveals that several public symptoms share the same runtime version.
- A rollback shelf waits beside an urgent repair card.
- Status lamps show support queues and update checks growing while public systems look calm.

## Canon Notes

- Service rooms may support the City Runtime, Civic Pages, Public Terminals, Queue District, Message Office, and other civic systems.
- They should not become a single magic room that explains every future incident.
- When service rooms appear in public fiction, name the specific service or dependency being investigated.
