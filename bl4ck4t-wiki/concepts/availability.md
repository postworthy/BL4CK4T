# Availability

## Summary

Availability means a service can be reached and used when people need it.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Security is not only keeping secrets.
- A service can harm people by failing to answer.
- Important services need fallback plans and honest status updates.
- Availability includes people waiting on the service.

## Story Hooks

- A clinic board spins while no records are missing.
- Whiskers learns to say what is working and what is unknown.
- Jinx adds waiting citizens to the Service Map.
