# Ethical Hacker Mindset

## Summary

The ethical hacker mindset in BL4CK4T is curiosity plus responsibility. The point is to learn, help, and defend.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

Use this concept to keep stories focused on consent, care for affected people, and learning that improves systems rather than exploiting them.

## Story Hooks

- A character stops a test before the exciting part.
- A report protects someone from blame.
- A clever shortcut is rejected because the boundary is wrong.

## Public Boundary

- Work with permission.
- Prefer safe labs and fictional systems.
- Explain impact without enabling harm.
- Pair each skill with a defensive use.
- Treat reporting and repair as part of the adventure.
