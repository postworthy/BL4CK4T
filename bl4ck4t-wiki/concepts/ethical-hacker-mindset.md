# Ethical Hacker Mindset

## Summary

The ethical hacker mindset in BL4CK4T is curiosity plus responsibility. The point is to learn, help, and defend.

## Key Points

- Work with permission.
- Prefer safe labs and fictional systems.
- Explain impact without enabling harm.
- Pair each skill with a defensive use.
- Treat reporting and repair as part of the adventure.
