# Proportionality

## Summary

Proportionality means matching response to evidence, harm, risk, and responsibility. Season 4 uses it to keep pursuit from becoming panic.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Harm matters.
- Evidence matters.
- Fear does not set the punishment.
- Accountability should protect people and stay tied to facts.

## Story Hooks

- The Glass Bureau asks for proof before citywide action.
- Whiskers refuses to let a rumor become a sentence.
- The team closes the chase by naming what changed, what remains unknown, and what repair requires.
