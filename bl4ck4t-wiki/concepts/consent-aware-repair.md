# Consent-Aware Repair

## Summary

Consent-aware repair fixes systems through authorized, visible, accountable paths rather than through surprise entry or forced changes.

## BL4CK4T Teaching Frame

- Repair is not only technical; it is relational.
- Owners and caretakers need to know what changed.
- A good repair path leaves records, rollback options, and trust intact.

## Story Hooks

- Consent Ledger entries.
- Repair Lane stamps.
- Byte choosing not to copy the Helpful Ghost's shortcut.
