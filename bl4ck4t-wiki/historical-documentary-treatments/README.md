# Historical Documentary Treatments

This directory stores documentary-style narrative treatments derived from completed historical accounts.

Rule: a documentary treatment comes after the historical account and before fictional adaptation. It may shape the factual material into dramatic movements, but it must remain nonfictional, source-aware, and clear about uncertainty.
