# Documentary Treatment: ILOVEYOU / The Love Bug

## Purpose

This treatment turns the historical account into a nonfiction movement structure for later adaptation. It sharpens the story of trust, curiosity, attachment handling, global spread, response, and legal aftermath without adding fictional material.

## Source Boundary

Use the historical account, source page, CERT/NIST material, contemporary reporting, and carefully qualified later accounts. Do not add fictional adaptation material here.

## Documentary Thesis

ILOVEYOU spread because it made unsafe action feel personal, familiar, and ordinary. Its lasting lesson is that security depends on human trust, software defaults, and response culture as much as technical controls.

## Cold Open

A message arrives from someone familiar. The subject feels personal. The attachment looks like a letter. The first seconds are not about code; they are about trust.

## Movement Structure

### Movement 1: A Message People Wanted To Believe

- Historical function: establish the lure.
- Narrative question: why did so many people open it?
- Key beats: familiar sender, love-letter theme, attachment curiosity.
- Evidence anchors: CERT advisory, contemporary reporting.
- Visual/audio language: inboxes, subject lines, office chatter, early morning alerts.
- Uncertainty guardrails: avoid blaming users with modern hindsight.

### Movement 2: Trust Becomes Transport

- Historical function: explain spread through contact relationships.
- Narrative question: how did personal trust become global scale?
- Key beats: address books, outbound messages, mail server strain.
- Evidence anchors: CERT and NIST.
- Visual/audio language: address books opening like maps, mail queues filling.
- Uncertainty guardrails: do not include reproducible behavior.

### Movement 3: Work Stops For A Letter

- Historical function: show victims and responders.
- Narrative question: what did organizations have to do first?
- Key beats: disabled mail, filtering, cleanup, damaged files, user warnings.
- Evidence anchors: contemporary reporting, advisories.
- Visual/audio language: dark mailrooms, phones ringing, help desks writing warnings.
- Uncertainty guardrails: caveat damage numbers.

### Movement 4: The Trail Reaches Manila

- Historical function: introduce investigation and attribution.
- Narrative question: what could investigators prove and what remained complicated?
- Key beats: suspected origin, local investigation, international attention.
- Evidence anchors: contemporary reporting and later careful summaries.
- Visual/audio language: press cameras, paper files, computer shops, legal offices.
- Uncertainty guardrails: qualify motive and legal details.

### Movement 5: Law Catches Up Late

- Historical function: explain legal aftermath.
- Narrative question: what happens when harm outruns statute?
- Key beats: legal gaps, policy discussion, cybercrime law pressure.
- Evidence anchors: legal and policy reporting.
- Visual/audio language: statutes, calendars, newsprint.
- Uncertainty guardrails: avoid claiming legal simplicity.

### Movement 6: What Email Learned

- Historical function: explain long-term significance.
- Narrative question: what changed after the outbreak?
- Key beats: filtering, safer defaults, user education, reporting habits.
- Evidence anchors: NIST, security retrospectives.
- Visual/audio language: warning banners, quarantines, training rooms.
- Uncertainty guardrails: do not claim it solved social engineering.

## Recurring Motifs

- The letter: intimacy, curiosity, and deception.
- The address book: trust becoming scale.
- The quarantine tray: response and containment.
- The warning: public communication under pressure.

## Interview Targets Or Archival Voices

- CERT/NIST advisories.
- Contemporary administrators and help-desk voices from reporting.
- Legal and policy sources on cybercrime-law gaps.
- Later retrospectives on email security and social engineering.

## Narration Guardrails

- Claims to state plainly: global spread, email attachment lure, address-book spread, major disruption.
- Claims to qualify: exact damages, motive, legal attribution, copycat variants.
- Claims to avoid: magical malware language, blame-the-user framing, operational reproduction.

## Ending

End with the permanent lesson: the most powerful path was not force. It was trust moving too quickly for caution.
