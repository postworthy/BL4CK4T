# Candidate: WannaCry Ransomware

## Executive Summary

WannaCry is a strong future-season candidate because it combines global ransomware spread, patch debt, public-service disruption, emergency response, and recovery. It gives BL4CK4T a way to tell a story where the visible harm is not a mysterious villain speech, but locked work, delayed care, stalled queues, and the painful cost of unpatched systems.

## Why This Is Season-Worthy

- It affected organizations around the world and became one of the most visible ransomware events of 2017.
- It connects technical vulnerability management to human consequences.
- It teaches patching, backups, network exposure, emergency response, and recovery.
- It gives the series a strong bridge from Season 3's recovery work and Season 4's public communication habits.

## Historical Scope

- Time period: 2017 outbreak and aftermath.
- Core event: ransomware spread through exposed and vulnerable systems, encrypting files and disrupting operations.
- Impact: hospitals, businesses, public services, and many ordinary users faced locked systems and emergency response pressure.
- Aftermath: renewed attention to patching, unsupported systems, backups, segmentation, ransomware readiness, and government response.

## Fit With BL4CK4T Continuity

- Builds naturally on the First Bell Desk, Restore Slips, and Byte's builder checklist.
- Lets Whiskers lead under public pressure while Grimalkin coordinates recovery lanes.
- Gives Byte a chance to connect patching and recovery to real human impact.

## BL4CK4T Transformation Potential

Cybertropolis wakes to frozen project lockers, stalled clinic kiosks, and locked classroom tools. The incident does not need a theatrical villain; the tension comes from restoration, prioritization, and the question of why old fixes were never applied.

## Likely Character Leads

- Byte: learns that maintenance is part of building.
- Whiskers: coordinates public calm and triage.
- Grimalkin: plans recovery lanes and service priority.
- Cipher: explains patch state and version drift.

## Likely Villains Or Forces

- Patch Phantom could return as the face of neglected updates.
- A ransomware force could appear as an incident pressure rather than a personified mastermind.

## Likely Concepts And Lore Needed

- Ransomware expansion page or deeper ransomware concept.
- Patch debt.
- Backups and restore testing.
- Service prioritization.
- Critical public-service recovery.

## Possible Episode Arc

1. A locked project locker appears.
2. The team learns the problem is spreading through old doors, not one bad choice.
3. Cipher maps patched and unpatched systems.
4. Whiskers coordinates which services return first.
5. Byte learns that a backup that was never tested is only a hope.
6. The city practices restore drills.
7. Patch Phantom's old neglect pattern becomes visible.
8. The team restores the last critical rooms.
9. Cybertropolis adopts maintenance days and recovery rehearsals.

## Risks And Handling Notes

- Risk: turning the season into a ransomware how-to.
- Handling: show locked systems, patch status, response coordination, and recovery without exploit or encryption mechanics.
- Risk: flattening public-service victims into scenery.
- Handling: give affected desks, caretakers, and students specific needs and agency.
