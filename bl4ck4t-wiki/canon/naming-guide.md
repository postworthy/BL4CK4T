# Naming Guide

## Summary

BL4CK4T names should feel playful, readable, and cyber-adjacent without becoming obscure. Names should help young readers remember the lesson.

## Drops

- Use short mission phrases from BL4CK4T.
- Prefer a puzzle-like sentence over a formal assignment.
- Good pattern: "The signal smiles before it bites."

## Missions

- Use article-like names with a story object and lesson hint.
- Good pattern: `The Phishmonger On Signal Row`.

## Villains

- Use fictional names that suggest behavior, not real actor identity.
- Good pattern: `The Phishmonger`, `Patch Phantom`, `Supply Serpent`.
- Avoid direct real APT names in public story titles.

## Artifacts

- Make technical objects concrete in Cybertropolis.
- Examples: mirror badges for spoofed identity, keylight locks for certificates, packet carts for dependencies, patch lanterns for updates.

## Districts

- District names should imply function.
- Examples: Signal Row, The Old Stack, Keylight Gate, Packet Market.
