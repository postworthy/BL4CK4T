# Historical Grounding Policy

## Summary

Any BL4CK4T season based on a real historical cybersecurity event must begin with an accurate, sourced historical account. The project should understand the real event deeply before transforming it into fiction.

## Required Order

1. Identify the real historical event, campaign, or era.
2. Gather authoritative and corroborating sources.
3. Write a historical account in `bl4ck4t-wiki/historical-accounts/`.
4. Separate sourced facts from uncertainty, dispute, and inference.
5. Create a nonfiction documentary treatment in `bl4ck4t-wiki/historical-documentary-treatments/` to shape the verified history into narrative movements without fictional adaptation.
6. Only then decompose the historical account and documentary treatment into BL4CK4T-world story material.
7. Create season transformations, villains, missions, drafts, and public articles after the historical account and documentary treatment are complete.

## Historical Account Standard

A historical account should include:

- Executive summary.
- Source list with direct URLs, publication/source names, and source type.
- Chronology.
- Key actors and institutions.
- Technical background.
- Narrative account of what happened.
- Consequences and historical significance.
- Disputed, uncertain, or inferred claims.
- Source-backed claim table.
- Open questions and research gaps.

Each account must also follow the [Historical Journalism Standard](../style-guides/historical-journalism-standard.md), including the separation of known facts, allegations, reasonable inferences, and unknowns.

## Source Standard

Prefer primary and authoritative sources:

- Court records, indictments, public reports, advisories, official postmortems, agency alerts, standards bodies, academic papers, reputable books, and direct researcher writeups.
- Use journalism and documentaries as secondary narrative support.
- Avoid relying on single-source summaries for contested claims.

## Story Transformation Rule

The historical account is not the story. It is the factual foundation. Project-specific transformation notes must live outside `bl4ck4t-wiki/historical-accounts/` so they do not contaminate the historical record.

## Documentary Treatment Rule

The documentary treatment is the bridge between factual research and adaptation. It should remain nonfictional, source-aware, and historically disciplined, but it may organize the history into cold opens, movements, narrative questions, visual motifs, interview targets, and narration guardrails. Fictional adaptation still happens after this step, not inside the treatment.
