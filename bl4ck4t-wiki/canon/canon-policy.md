# Canon Policy

## Summary

BL4CK4T uses soft canon by default. Existing public posts establish the foundation of the world, characters, tone, and lessons, but future worldbuilding may refine details when needed for consistency.

## Canon Levels

- Hard canon: published article titles, core lesson outcomes, named Script Kitties, BL4CK4T as the unseen mentor, and Cybertropolis as the setting.
- Soft canon: exact phrasing, incidental scene details, undeveloped backstory, visual details, and character traits that have not yet been reinforced across multiple stories.
- Draft canon: wiki-only ideas, villain backlog entries, planned arcs, and unapproved article drafts.

## Revision Rule

When a new idea conflicts with existing material, preserve the tension in a canon or source page before rewriting continuity. Public content should not be changed retroactively without user approval.
