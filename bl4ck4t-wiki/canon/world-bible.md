# World Bible

## Summary

BL4CK4T is a neon-cyberpunk learning world where cybersecurity, math, ethics, and practical curiosity become story missions. The Script Kitties receive mysterious drops from BL4CK4T, decode the challenge, learn a real concept, test it through playful trial and error, and leave with a lesson that applies beyond the immediate puzzle.

## Current World Rules

- Cybertropolis is the primary setting.
- BL4CK4T is an unseen mentor who sends drops through glowing pawprints, encrypted messages, billboards, simulators, and other city signals.
- The Script Kitties are learners, not criminals. Their work is framed around curiosity, consent, defense, and growth.
- Each mission should teach a real concept through a fictionalized challenge.
- Stories should feel playful and adventurous while keeping the lesson accurate enough for beginners.
- The world can transform real cybersecurity history into stylized fiction, but should not expose harmful operational detail.
- Public stories should keep the stakes understandable: lost trust, locked files, confused signals, broken city services, or unsafe shortcuts.
- BL4CK4T can set up labs, clues, simulations, and safe city-scale puzzles, but should not simply solve the problem for the team.
- The Script Kitties should win by observing, asking better questions, testing safely, and explaining what defenders can do.

## Current Story Pattern

1. A BL4CK4T drop appears.
2. The team identifies the puzzle or concept.
3. Different Script Kitties test ideas according to their personalities.
4. The lesson becomes clear through trial, teamwork, and analogy.
5. BL4CK4T closes the mission with a final message.

## Reader Assumptions

- Readers may be new to cybersecurity.
- Stories should assume curiosity, not prior tool knowledge.
- Public technical detail should be lab-safe, defensive, and explainable to teens and teachers.

## Public-Safe Material

- Definitions, analogies, defensive habits, safe lab references, historical context, and high-level TTP explanations are allowed.
- Real exploit chains, malware construction, evasion, credential theft instructions, and target-specific abuse details are not public story material.

## Core Districts

- [Signal Row](../locations/cybertropolis.md#signal-row) - message trust, phishing, social engineering, alerts, broadcasts.
- [The Old Stack](../locations/cybertropolis.md#the-old-stack) - legacy systems, patching, forgotten services, maintenance.
- [Keylight Gate](../locations/cybertropolis.md#keylight-gate) - identity, cryptography, keys, signatures, certificates.
- [Packet Market](../locations/cybertropolis.md#packet-market) - dependencies, packages, tools, supply chain, provenance.
