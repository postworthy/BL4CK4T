# Hero Anchor Policy

## Summary

Each major BL4CK4T character has a hero or persona anchor. The anchor is an ethos guide, not a template to copy. The Script Kitties are younger characters whose personalities are still forming, so their backstories should echo the spirit, virtues, flaws, and growth direction of their anchors without reproducing the full depth, history, fame, trauma, or adult formation of those anchors.

## Core Rule

Use anchors to steer development over time:

- Preserve the BL4CK4T character's existing role and voice.
- Borrow the anchor's spirit, not their biography.
- Keep backstories age-appropriate and formative.
- Let stories gradually grow the character toward the anchor's mature virtues.
- Avoid direct parody, direct renaming, costume copying, or one-to-one plot duplication.

## Selected Anchors

- [Whiskers](../characters/whiskers.md) - Captain America / Steve Rogers: principled team leadership, responsibility, moral courage.
- [Grimalkin](../characters/grimalkin.md) - Batman / Bruce Wayne: preparation, threat modeling, discipline, caution, adaptive planning.
- [Pixel](../characters/pixel.md) - Richard Feynman: playful curiosity, hands-on explanation, joyful experimentation, making hard ideas intuitive.
- [Byte](../characters/byte.md) - MacGyver: practical invention, improvisation, constraints, non-destructive problem solving.
- [Cipher](../characters/cipher.md) - Alan Turing plus Ada Lovelace: codebreaking logic, formal reasoning, mathematical imagination, seeing what machines can become.
- [Jinx](../characters/jinx.md) - Nancy Drew: investigation, clue tracking, persistence, skepticism of easy answers.
- [Shadow](../characters/shadow.md) - T'Challa / Black Panther: quiet discipline, observation, principled restraint, protective competence.
- [BL4CK4T](../characters/bl4ck4t.md) - Gandalf: mysterious mentor, indirect guidance, challenge-based growth, benevolent restraint.

## Backstory Development Guidance

Character backstories should start with small, formative versions of the anchor traits:

- A leadership mistake, not a battlefield.
- A planning habit, not a lifelong crusade.
- A curiosity spark, not a scientific legacy.
- A repaired toy or classroom tool, not a world-saving device.
- A solved puzzle, not a historic codebreaking operation.
- A neighborhood mystery, not a famous case file.
- A quiet act of protection, not a throne.
- A cryptic mentor choice, not a mythic war.

## Continuity Note

Anchors should influence character arcs across many stories. A single article does not need to reveal a full backstory. Prefer small moments that slowly show why the character is becoming who they are.
