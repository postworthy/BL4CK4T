# Keys To The City Lesson

## Summary

This lesson teaches public/private key cryptography and shared-secret intuition through mailboxes, signatures, toy RSA, and Diffie-Hellman color mixing.

## Teaching Payload

- Concept: asymmetric key pairs.
- Analogy: public mailbox slot and private mailbox key.
- Activity idea: color-mixing exchange for shared secrets.
- Extension: signing with a private key and verifying with a public key.
- Takeaway: modern trust systems rely on carefully paired keys.

## Story Use

Use this lesson when a mission involves trust, identity, messages in the open, impostors, locked city gates, or secure channels.
