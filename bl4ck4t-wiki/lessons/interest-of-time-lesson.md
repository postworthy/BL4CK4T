# Interest Of Time Lesson

## Summary

This lesson teaches compound interest as a force shaped by time, rate, consistency, and debt.

## Teaching Payload

- Concept: compound interest.
- Contrast: simple interest grows linearly; compound interest bends upward.
- Activity idea: simulator panels for early start, frequency, rate, debt, small principal, and Rule of 72.
- Takeaway: time and consistency can transform small choices into large outcomes.
