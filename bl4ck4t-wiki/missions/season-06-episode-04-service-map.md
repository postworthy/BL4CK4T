# Mission: The Service Map

## Summary

Jinx builds the Service Map and discovers it is incomplete because it lists systems but not the people waiting on them. Whiskers sees that dependency mapping is not only technical. It is civic.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the public Season 6 release is approved.

## Historical Documentary Anchor

- Movement 2: civic dependence on online systems.
- Movement 4: coordinated triage among service operators, media, banks, government, and outside helpers.

## Featured Characters

- Jinx: turns evidence mapping into dependency mapping.
- Whiskers: begins seeing leadership as care for people waiting, not only finding the source.

## Location

- Queue District workroom and the emerging [Service Map](../artifacts/service-map.md).

## Villain Or Conflict

- The conflict is incomplete understanding. The city cannot prioritize what it has not mapped.

## Opening Control

Open on Jinx revising the map because a person is missing from it. Do not open with a failed service.

## Story Beats

1. Jinx draws services, counters, and routing lamps.
2. Whiskers asks where a delayed clinic patient appears on the map.
3. Jinx realizes a dependency map without people hides the actual harm.
4. The team marks essential services, pausable services, and unknown dependencies.
5. A quiet service that still works becomes as important as a failed one.
6. BL4CK4T's drop asks who disappears when a map only shows machines.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Civic Resilience](../concepts/civic-resilience.md).
- Defensive takeaway: responders need to know what depends on what before a crisis.
- Teacher note: make the map a story object, not a diagram lecture.

## Teaching Tie-In Target

- Concept: Dependency mapping.
- Story idea: the Service Map changes when people are added.
- Key distinction: mapping systems is not the same as mapping impact.
- Defensive habit: identify critical dependencies before they fail.
- Season thread: availability is part of trust.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Transform national digital-dependence analysis into a city service map.
- Keep the artifact reusable for later seasons.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not include real infrastructure diagrams or target-like details.
- Keep the map conceptual and fictional.

## Prose Quality Warning

Avoid letting this become a static planning meeting. The map should change because a concrete person or service forces a revision.

## Episode Hook

Jinx draws a perfect line between two service desks, and Whiskers asks where the waiting child goes.
