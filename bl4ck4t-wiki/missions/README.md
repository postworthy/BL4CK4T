# Missions

Mission pages are pre-draft story packets. They connect canon, characters, locations, villains, lessons, and source inspiration before a blog article is drafted.

Create a mission before creating an article draft when a story introduces new lore, a villain, a real-world inspiration, or a reusable lesson.

Minimum mission requirements:

- Links to at least one lesson or concept page.
- Names the featured characters.
- Names the location.
- Describes the safe public transformation.
- Lists safety constraints.
