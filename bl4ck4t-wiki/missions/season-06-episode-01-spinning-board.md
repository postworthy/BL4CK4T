# Mission: The Spinning Board

## Summary

Season 6 opens inside an ordinary civic consequence: a clinic or school service board spins while people wait for something small but important. The Script Kitties do not begin with a villain, a clue board, or a technical explanation. They begin with people who need the city to answer.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the public Season 6 release is approved.

## Historical Documentary Anchor

- Cold Open: the country cannot be reliably reached.
- Movement 2: digital civic dependence.
- Movement 3: the first silence and availability-focused disruption.

## Featured Characters

- Whiskers: feels immediate pressure to explain what is happening before he knows enough.
- Shadow: watches who is waiting, who is quietly helped, and which public systems blink instead of answering.

## Location

- A clinic or school waiting area, with the Service Bell Tower heard in the distance.
- Status Wall only appears near the end as a place Whiskers will need to use honestly.

## Villain Or Conflict

- The Flood Prince is not named yet. The conflict is absence: no dramatic message, no stolen file, only a board that will not answer.

## Opening Control

Open from the perspective of an ordinary person waiting on a civic service. Do not open with the team arriving at a room, reading a drop, or standing before a map.

## Story Beats

1. A small public service hangs long enough to matter.
2. The Message Office reports empty receipts still flooding in after Season 5's close.
3. Whiskers starts to answer too quickly, then stops because the answer would be a guess.
4. Shadow notices that not every room is affected equally.
5. The first Service Bell rings for a degraded service, not a destroyed one.
6. BL4CK4T's drop points to the difference between a locked chest and a blocked door.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Availability](../concepts/availability.md).
- Defensive takeaway: a service that cannot answer can still cause harm even when no data is stolen.
- Teacher note: make waiting concrete through one human consequence.

## Teaching Tie-In Target

- Concept: Availability.
- Story idea: the city is harmed by delay and silence, not theft.
- Key distinction: unavailable is different from corrupted.
- Defensive habit: notice and report service failure clearly.
- Season thread: availability is part of trust.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Transform Estonia's public-service disruption into Cybertropolis civic waiting rooms and status boards.
- Preserve nuance: the city is strained, not fully paralyzed.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not describe how floods are generated.
- Keep attention on service impact, triage, and clear reporting.

## Prose Quality Warning

This episode must not use the stock "team gathers at the Hideout" opening. Its value is the cold open inside consequence.

## Episode Hook

The clinic board says `Answering...` for so long that the room learns the word by heart.
