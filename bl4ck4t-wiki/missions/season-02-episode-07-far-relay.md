# Mission: The Far Relay

## Summary

The team finds signs that borrowed access is feeding a brokered channel called the Far Relay. The Ledgerjack appears as a mark and rumor, not as a mastermind. The episode widens the stakes while keeping the case grounded in evidence.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the Season 2 episode set is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Jinx: faces the difference between curiosity and selling what belongs to others.
- Whiskers: prevents the wider discovery from becoming panic.
- Byte: keeps the Threadboard faithful to the record.
- BL4CK4T: drops `TRACE THE THREAD. DO NOT PULL THE CITY APART.`

## Location

- Ledger Lab and external Threadboard traces.

## Villain Or Conflict

The Far Relay is the wider adversary layer. Ledgerjack is an indirect broker identity, not the source of every incident.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Account Misuse](../concepts/account-misuse.md) and evidence-based escalation.
- Defensive takeaway: widening a case requires discipline.
- Teacher note: distinguish curiosity, intrusion, and selling other people's work.

## Real-World Inspiration

- The historical escalation from local intrusion evidence to a wider espionage and market context.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Historical people and governments are replaced by fictional broker channels.
- Keep the adversary less glamorous than the investigation.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not show marketplaces, contacts, transaction mechanics, or real-world analogues.
- Keep all action on defender interpretation and escalation.

## Episode Hook

A new card appears on the Threadboard with no name, only a jagged ledger mark stamped in gray.
