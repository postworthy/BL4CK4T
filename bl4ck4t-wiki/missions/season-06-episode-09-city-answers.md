# Mission: The City Answers

## Summary

The flood subsides, but the finale does not stop at relief. The city keeps the Status Wall, reviews the Service Map, records what changed, and discovers one remaining red mark that points to old patch debt for Season 7.

2. Citizens return to services that now display clearer status.

5. The team marks resolved, degraded, watched, and unknown services.
6. One red mark remains after the flood ends.
7. Grimalkin identifies it as old known repair debt, creating the Season 7 bridge.
