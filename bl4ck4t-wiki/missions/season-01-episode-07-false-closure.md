# Mission: The False Closure

## Summary

A Signal Row kiosk posts a false closure notice and sends a crowd toward the wrong transit platform. Jinx and Shadow trace the evidence while Whiskers keeps the team focused on containment instead of blame.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the Season 1 episode set is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Jinx: leads the evidence trail.
- Shadow: finds the timing mismatch and physical clue.
- Whiskers: chooses containment over accusation.
- Pixel: feels the emotional shift after the first real consequence.
- Byte: helps compare the false notice with the Tonebox model.

## Location

- Signal Row transit kiosks, platform notices, and relay cabinet corridor.

## Villain Or Conflict

The conflict is the first concrete consequence of copycat behavior. The actor remains unclear.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: evidence, impact, and containment, connected to [Ethical Hacker Mindset](../concepts/ethical-hacker-mindset.md).
- Defensive takeaway: when something goes wrong, stabilize the situation before chasing blame.
- Teacher note: this episode turns the season's mystery into an incident without becoming catastrophic.

## Real-World Inspiration

- Legal and civic consequences in the blue-box era, transformed into a fictional public-service disruption.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Real toll fraud and telecom disruption become a false civic notice.
- The story focuses on impact, evidence, and response.
- The copycat remains unnamed to keep the focus on system behavior and rumor.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not include operational detail.
- Show investigation and containment, not replication.
- Keep the incident serious but not disastrous.

## Episode Hook

A kiosk posts `SOUTH TUBE CLOSED` for twelve minutes even though the line is open, and Jinx is the first to notice the timing does not fit a normal outage.
