# Mission: The Outside Gate

## Summary

Some Cybertropolis services restrict outside requests so local clinics, schools, and public notices can keep answering. Grimalkin and Shadow must handle the fairness problem: a useful emergency measure can also leave legitimate people outside the gate.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the public Season 6 release is approved.

## Historical Documentary Anchor

- Movement 4: foreign-access restrictions and selective widening to keep essential domestic services reachable.
- Movement 5: internet borders do not equal responsibility or identity.

## Featured Characters

- Grimalkin: frames temporary restriction as a careful triage choice with review.
- Shadow: watches who is harmed by the restriction and keeps the team from treating outsiders as a single suspect group.

## Location

- The outside gate of the Queue District and the Status Wall's access notices.

## Villain Or Conflict

- The conflict is constrained access. The city must keep essential service reachable without turning geography, district, or origin into blame.

## Opening Control

Open from someone outside Cybertropolis trying to reach a public notice for a legitimate reason. Do not open inside the team's control room.

## Story Beats

1. A distant reader cannot reach a public notice that matters to them.
2. Inside the city, the same restriction keeps a clinic board answering locally.
3. Shadow refuses to call every outside request hostile.
4. Grimalkin creates temporary signs that explain restriction, reason, and review.
5. The team opens a narrow priority path for verified civic needs without pretending the tradeoff is painless.
6. BL4CK4T's drop asks who pays when a gate must narrow.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Civic Resilience](../concepts/civic-resilience.md).
- Defensive takeaway: emergency restrictions need purpose, communication, and review.
- Teacher note: preserve moral complexity without operational detail.

## Teaching Tie-In Target

- Concept: Resilience under constrained access.
- Story idea: narrowing a gate can protect service while creating new access problems.
- Key distinction: outside is not the same as hostile.
- Defensive habit: explain temporary limits and review them often.
- Season thread: availability is part of trust.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Transform historical foreign-traffic restrictions into fictional outside-gate triage.
- Avoid blaming outsiders as a group.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not describe network filtering steps.
- Keep emphasis on fairness, priority, and communication.

## Prose Quality Warning

Make the outside perspective sympathetic. This episode fails if restriction feels like an easy win.

## Episode Hook

The notice was public, the need was real, and the gate still would not open.
