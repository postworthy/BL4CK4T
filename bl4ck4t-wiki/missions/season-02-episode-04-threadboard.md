# Mission: The Threadboard

## Summary

Byte and Jinx build the Threadboard, a defensive evidence wall that orders records without changing the scene. Whiskers pushes for containment while Jinx argues for enough observation to make the case useful.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the Season 2 episode set is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Jinx: learns patience under pressure.
- Byte: builds a tool for preservation and comparison.
- Whiskers: balances protection with evidence quality.

## Location

- The Hideout: Threadboard construction and case review.
- Ledger Lab: source of records and print slips.

## Villain Or Conflict

The conflict is pressure to act before the team understands the thread's shape.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Evidence Preservation](../concepts/evidence-preservation.md).
- Defensive takeaway: good defensive tools keep the record clear.
- Teacher note: show that preserving evidence can be active work.

## Real-World Inspiration

- Improvised logging and evidence building in early intrusion investigations.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Logs become tactile cards, clipped slips, and timeline lights.
- Defensive instrumentation stays observational.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not create a trap, counterattack, or surveillance fantasy.
- Keep collection consented through caretakers and local records.

## Episode Hook

The Threadboard lights for the first time, and the smallest card on it is still the red `-0.75`.
