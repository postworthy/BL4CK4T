# Mission: The Investigator's Case

## Summary

Jinx turns the Threadboard into a case others can act on: what was known first, what changed, what was ruled out, what remains unknown, and what the evidence supports. The Glass Bureau and Grid caretakers can finally move with confidence.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the Season 2 episode set is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Jinx: completes the suspicion-to-evidence arc.
- Grimalkin: presents alternate theories that were tested and removed.
- Byte: demonstrates the Threadboard as an evidence instrument.
- Whiskers: helps the team speak clearly to responders.

## Location

- The Hideout: final case assembly.
- Glass Bureau review table.

## Villain Or Conflict

The conflict is whether the team can make a complex record legible without overstating it.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Evidence Preservation](../concepts/evidence-preservation.md) and case building.
- Defensive takeaway: evidence is strongest when uncertainty is explicit.
- Teacher note: emphasize chronology and tested alternatives.

## Real-World Inspiration

- Defender-as-detective work in historical intrusion investigations.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Documentary case presentation becomes a BL4CK4T-world evidence briefing.
- Keep uncertainty visible and useful.

## Safety Constraints

- No operational details.
- Avoid naming unsupported conclusions.

## Episode Hook

Jinx removes three dramatic cards from the Threadboard because the case is stronger without them.
