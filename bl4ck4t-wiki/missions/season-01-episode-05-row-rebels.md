# Mission: The Row Rebels

## Summary

The Script Kitties meet the Row Rebels, a loose group of curious Signal Row kids and makers who believe the old signal layer belongs to everyone. Pixel sympathizes with their wonder, but Jinx and Shadow notice fake alerts and confused citizens.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the Season 1 episode set is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Whiskers: sees the civic stakes and has to lead without flattening curiosity.
- Jinx: tracks fake notices and public confusion.
- Pixel: understands why the Row Rebels feel inspired.
- Shadow: catches subtle changes in public signs.
- Cipher: separates protest slogans from system behavior.

## Location

- Signal Row, with special focus on public kiosks, transit notices, and Row Rebel sticker paths.

## Villain Or Conflict

The Row Rebels are not villains. The conflict is the blur between curiosity, protest, status, and disruption.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: ethical hacker mindset, connected to [Ethical Hacker Mindset](../concepts/ethical-hacker-mindset.md).
- Defensive takeaway: intent matters, but impact decides whether a public system has been bent too far.
- Teacher note: this episode lets the team disagree about a gray-area group without losing empathy.

## Real-World Inspiration

- Countercultural and anti-system dimensions of phone-phreaking history, transformed into fictional Signal Row peer culture.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Real political and underground communities become the Row Rebels.
- Real infrastructure abuse becomes fictional fake notices and minor civic disruption.
- The story keeps the Rebels human and mixed-motive.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not include operational details.
- Show consequences through confused signs and crowd movement, not technical procedure.
- Keep the Row Rebels complicated rather than purely villainous.

## Episode Hook

The Script Kitties find a Row Rebel gathering under a flickering billboard where everyone is arguing whether the city can be owned if everyone can hear it.
