# Mission: Nothing Missing

## Summary

Pixel and Cipher inspect ledgers, receipts, and public-service records expecting to find a changed entry. They find a stranger clue: nothing is missing, nothing is rewritten, and people are still being harmed.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the public Season 6 release is approved.

## Historical Documentary Anchor

- Movement 3: DoS/DDoS as availability-focused pressure.
- Movement 4: responders must act before the whole picture is clear.

## Featured Characters

- Pixel: wants a visible broken thing and struggles when the clue is absence.
- Cipher: helps name the difference between changed records and blocked access.

## Location

- Message Office receipt ledger and a small records room near the Status Wall.

## Villain Or Conflict

- The conflict is conceptual: the team has to stop measuring harm only by theft or alteration.

## Opening Control

Open in a too-orderly records room where every ledger balances. Do not repeat Episode 1's waiting-room opening.

## Story Beats

1. Pixel expects a monster mark, copied letter, or altered record.
2. Cipher confirms the records are intact.
3. The blank receipts continue arriving in bursts.
4. A clerk explains that intact records do not help when nobody can submit the next form.
5. Pixel realizes absence can be evidence.
6. BL4CK4T's drop asks what is broken when the lock is fine but the doorway is packed shut.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Denial Of Service](../concepts/denial-of-service.md).
- Defensive takeaway: availability incidents can matter even without data theft.
- Teacher note: keep the mechanism plain and non-operational.

## Teaching Tie-In Target

- Concept: Denial of service.
- Story idea: the records are safe, but the city cannot use them.
- Key distinction: nothing missing does not mean nothing wrong.
- Defensive habit: record symptoms and impact, not only missing files.
- Season thread: availability is part of trust.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Use blank receipts and blocked doorways instead of real traffic mechanics.
- Keep the story focused on how defenders identify the class of harm.

## Safety Constraints

- Avoid technical reproduction details.
- Do not show commands, tools, or request-generation methods.

## Prose Quality Warning

Avoid restating Episode 1's waiting-people imagery. This episode should feel like an evidence-room puzzle where the puzzle is that the evidence is clean.

Pixel finds the neatest ledger he has ever seen, and that is what bothers Cipher.
