# Mission: The Sound Beneath Signal Row

## Summary

Pixel hears a strange three-note tone under Signal Row after the district settles into maintenance mode. Shadow notices that the same tone makes an old rooftop beacon flicker. The team follows the clue to archived civic diagrams and finds the first hint of the Echo Grid, an old signal layer beneath the city.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the Season 1 episode set is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Pixel: hears the tone first and gives the episode its sense of wonder.
- Cipher: recognizes structure in the sound and pushes the team to map before acting.
- Shadow: notices the beacon flicker and proves the tone touches something physical.
- Whiskers: keeps the team moving and asks what the city itself might do.
- Byte: builds the first tabletop model of message and command sharing one line.

## Location

- Signal Row: closing-time neon, old message kiosks, rooftop beacons, and forgotten routing cabinets.
- The Hideout: archive wall and first tabletop Echo Grid model.

## Villain Or Conflict

No villain appears yet. The conflict is the mystery of the hidden signal and the team's temptation to answer it before they understand it.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: hidden signaling and system mapping, connected to [Network Discovery](../concepts/network-discovery.md) and the pattern-finding roots in [Hidden Code Lesson](../lessons/hidden-code-lesson.md).
- Defensive takeaway: observe first, map the system, then decide what action belongs in the story.
- Teacher note: this episode introduces the difference between an ordinary message and an instruction that affects system behavior.

## Real-World Inspiration

- Phone phreaking and the blue-box era, transformed through the Season 1 documentary treatment and story arc.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Real phone systems become the fictional Echo Grid.
- Real tones become a fictional three-note city motif.
- Real phreaking details are replaced by mystery, observation, and architecture.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not include real telecom tones, frequencies, devices, procedures, or historical abuse steps.
- Keep the team's action to observation, archive reading, and a disconnected tabletop model.
- Let the mystery carry the episode.

## Episode Hook

Signal Row goes dark for maintenance. Pixel hears a three-note phrase under the last neon buzz. A rooftop beacon answers it with one pale blink.
