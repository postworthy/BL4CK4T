# Mission: The Tonebox Demo

## Summary

Byte and Pixel refine the Tonebox into a captivating model that makes the Echo Grid's hidden rule visible. The demonstration works, but its beauty makes the idea easier to copy and distort.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the Season 1 episode set is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Byte: leads the build and learns that craft includes intent.
- Pixel: brings wonder and showmanship to the demo.
- Cipher: writes the model assumptions and keeps the explanation honest.
- Whiskers: questions how public the demo should become.
- Jinx: tracks how fast the demo gets misread.

## Location

- The Hideout Signal Lab.

## Villain Or Conflict

The conflict is the tension between making a mystery understandable and making it too easy to imitate badly.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: models, assumptions, and tool interpretation, connected to [Network Discovery](../concepts/network-discovery.md).
- Defensive takeaway: a model should make an idea clear without pretending to be the live system.
- Teacher note: this episode treats a technical demo as a story object with consequences.

## Real-World Inspiration

- The historical power of making hidden signaling portable, transformed into the fictional Tonebox.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Real devices become the fictional Tonebox model.
- The Tonebox is a lantern for understanding, not a city-control tool.
- The episode focuses on craft, assumptions, and distortion.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not include construction details that map to real systems.
- Keep the Tonebox disconnected and fictional.
- Let the copycat risk emerge through story rather than instructions.

## Episode Hook

The Tonebox makes the model city glow so beautifully that Pixel forgets, for one breath, why Whiskers was worried.
