# Mission: We Do Not Know Yet

## Summary

Whiskers must write the first public status update while the team still lacks full evidence. Cipher helps him separate known facts, observed impacts, active response, and unknowns. The episode turns Whiskers' fear of uncertainty into the season's leadership pivot.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the public Season 6 release is approved.

## Historical Documentary Anchor

- Movement 4: public communication during triage and uncertainty.
- Movement 5: attribution caution before proof.

## Featured Characters

- Whiskers: wants to sound certain and learns that honest uncertainty is stronger than a confident guess.
- Cipher: structures the status message so it says enough without pretending too much.

## Location

- [Status Wall](../locations/status-wall.md).

## Villain Or Conflict

- The conflict is pressure to overstate. Rumors move faster than evidence, and citizens want a single answer.

## Opening Control

Open with Whiskers drafting and crossing out overconfident status language. Do not open with another system symptom.

## Story Beats

1. Whiskers writes a strong-sounding explanation, then realizes it claims too much.
2. Citizens and clerks ask whether the flood is over, who caused it, and whether anything was stolen.
3. Cipher divides the message into known, unknown, and next update.
4. The Status Wall posts degraded, available, unavailable, and investigating categories.
5. Whiskers publicly says, `We do not know yet`, then follows it with what the city is doing.
6. BL4CK4T's drop reinforces that guessing loudly can make a quiet crisis worse.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Status Communication](../concepts/status-communication.md).
- Defensive takeaway: good incident updates distinguish facts, impact, action, and unknowns.
- Teacher note: make uncertainty disciplined, not vague.

## Teaching Tie-In Target

- Concept: Status communication.
- Story idea: Whiskers learns to tell the truth before the full answer is known.
- Key distinction: honest uncertainty is different from confusion.
- Defensive habit: communicate what is known, what is affected, what is being done, and when the next update comes.
- Season thread: availability is part of trust.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Transform public statements and defender uncertainty into a Status Wall scene.
- Avoid making attribution a dramatic reveal.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not identify real-world actors or mirror real attribution claims in fiction.
- Avoid operational language.

## Prose Quality Warning

This episode's drama is language under pressure. Keep it active through crossed-out drafts, public questions, and Whiskers' choice.

## Episode Hook

Whiskers writes `Everything is under control`, then stares at the sentence until it starts to look like a lie.
