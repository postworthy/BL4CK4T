# Mission: The Listeners' Marks

## Summary

The Script Kitties return to Signal Row and discover chalk marks, sticker codes, and rooftop scratches left by earlier signal-watchers. Pixel feels drawn to the hidden community. Jinx notices that one mark is new, and Shadow finds a fresh scratch near the old relay cabinet.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the Season 1 episode set is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Pixel: sees the old marks as proof that other curious listeners came before the team.
- Jinx: questions whether the marks are harmless memory or current activity.
- Shadow: finds the fresh clue that changes the marks from history to present tense.
- Cipher: helps sort mark patterns by age and repetition.
- Whiskers: keeps the team focused on what the marks prove.

## Location

- Signal Row rooftops, alleys, repair ladders, relay cabinets, and closed message kiosks.

## Villain Or Conflict

No named villain appears. The conflict is the discovery that the Echo Grid has a social history, and that someone may still be using the old listener marks.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: discovery communities, pattern collection, and evidence sorting, connected to [Network Discovery](../concepts/network-discovery.md).
- Defensive takeaway: a technical clue becomes stronger when its age, source, and context are checked.
- Teacher note: this episode shows that systems are also social spaces. Knowledge travels through marks, rumors, habits, and shared curiosity.

## Real-World Inspiration

- The historical role of early phreak communities and shared knowledge, transformed into Signal Row listener marks.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Historical phreak communities become fictional signal-watchers.
- Real conference lines and underground lore become chalk marks, stickers, and rooftop scratches.
- The episode focuses on community, evidence, and mystery rather than method.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not include real telecom details or phreaking procedure.
- Keep the marks fictional and non-operational.
- Let the fresh clue drive the story into Episode 3.

## Episode Hook

Pixel finds a faded mark that matches the rhythm of the three-note tone. Shadow finds the same mark freshly scratched into a cabinet door.
