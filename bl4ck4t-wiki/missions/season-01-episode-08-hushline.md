# Mission: The Hushline

## Summary

The team works with a retired Signal Row maintainer to understand the Hushline, a cleaner design that separates public messages from routing commands. Cipher and Byte build the split-path model while Whiskers coordinates the change.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the Season 1 episode set is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Cipher: explains the message-command split.
- Byte: builds the split-path model.
- Whiskers: coordinates the repair effort.
- Pixel: learns that a clearer city can still be beautiful.
- Jinx: checks that the proposed fix matches the evidence.

## Location

- Retired maintainer's repair shop.
- The Hideout Signal Lab.
- Signal Row relay corridor.

## Villain Or Conflict

The conflict is architectural: old cabinets still listen in the old way, and the team needs a better path before rumor causes another incident.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: separation of message and command paths, connected to [Network Discovery](../concepts/network-discovery.md) and [Keys To The City Lesson](../lessons/keys-to-the-city-lesson.md).
- Defensive takeaway: the best fix changes the structure so the old mistake cannot keep repeating.
- Teacher note: this episode turns the season's mystery into an architecture lesson.

## Real-World Inspiration

- Out-of-band/common-channel signaling as a historical fix, transformed into the fictional Hushline.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Historical signaling redesign becomes the Hushline.
- Real technical details become a fictional split-path city model.
- The episode focuses on architecture, not implementation.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not include real telecom implementation details.
- Keep the Hushline fictional.
- Let the model carry the idea.

## Episode Hook

A retired maintainer tells the team that Signal Row was never supposed to stay on the Echo Grid this long.
