# Mission: The Borrowed Door

## Summary

The team connects the recurring mismatch to after-hours activity that does not fit the project owner's schedule. Shadow spots the physical clue, Cipher checks the timing, and Grimalkin names a careful hypothesis: a borrowed door.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the Season 2 episode set is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Jinx: moves from intuition to demonstrable pattern.
- Shadow: notices the terminal and unclaimed print slip.
- Cipher: checks timing and repetition.
- Grimalkin: keeps alternate explanations alive.

## Location

- Ledger Lab: after-hours terminal row and print counter.

## Villain Or Conflict

The conflict is unauthorized use hiding inside familiar access. No culprit is named.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Account Misuse](../concepts/account-misuse.md) from the defender's view.
- Defensive takeaway: unusual timing and mismatched records can reveal misuse.
- Teacher note: explain that a system can recognize an account while the defender still has to ask who is really using it.

## Real-World Inspiration

- Intrusion investigations where legitimate-looking accounts become evidence questions.

## Public Transformation Notes

- The "borrowed door" stays a metaphor for defender-observed behavior.
- The episode avoids procedures and focuses on records and observation.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not show credential theft, login mechanics, or bypass steps.
- Keep the useful action to comparison, timing, and reporting.

## Episode Hook

Shadow touches the edge of a sleeping terminal and feels warmth that should not be there.
