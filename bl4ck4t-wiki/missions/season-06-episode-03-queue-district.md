# Mission: The Queue District

## Summary

Jinx and Grimalkin enter the Queue District and discover that public requests are not failing all at once. Some move slowly, some circle, some are answered locally, and some never reach the right desk. The city learns to distinguish degraded service from total outage.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the public Season 6 release is approved.

## Historical Documentary Anchor

- Movement 2: digital public services as civic infrastructure.
- Movement 4: triage while keeping essential domestic services reachable.

## Featured Characters

- Jinx: starts mapping service states instead of only suspect clues.
- Grimalkin: explains why partial service needs calmer procedures than panic.

## Location

- [Queue District](../locations/queue-district.md), where request slips, public counters, and answer desks make invisible service handling tangible.

## Villain Or Conflict

- The pressure is uneven load: some desks can answer, others are overwhelmed, and citizens cannot tell the difference without public status.

## Opening Control

Open with a single request token moving through too many desks. Do not open with a board spinning or a ledger inspection.

## Story Beats

1. A request token leaves one desk and returns with no answer.
2. Jinx tracks the token's path through the district.
3. Grimalkin separates unavailable, degraded, delayed, and locally reachable.
4. A clerk tries to help but gives three different explanations to three different people.
5. Jinx starts the first rough service-state list.
6. BL4CK4T's drop introduces the idea that a city must name delay honestly.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Service Degradation](../concepts/service-degradation.md).
- Defensive takeaway: partial failure still needs clear status and triage.
- Teacher note: avoid all-or-nothing language.

## Teaching Tie-In Target

- Concept: Service degradation.
- Story idea: some services answer slowly or only for some people.
- Key distinction: degraded is not the same as down.
- Defensive habit: describe impact precisely.
- Season thread: availability is part of trust.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Convert mitigation and routing pressure into fictional service desks and request tokens.
- Preserve the historical nuance that disruption varied by service and time.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not describe filtering mechanics in operational detail.
- Emphasize observation, classification, and communication.

## Prose Quality Warning

Keep the Queue District physical and specific. Avoid generic "the city was overwhelmed" narration without a concrete request path.

## Episode Hook

The request token comes back stamped `later`, `try again`, and `already answered`, all at once.
