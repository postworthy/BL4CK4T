# Mission: Notice Wall

## Summary

The City Chronicle prints a dramatic nickname for the caller. Jinx and Pixel mark every public claim as seen, guessed, repeated, or unknown.

## Canon Status

Public canon once Season 4 publishes.

## Featured Characters

- Jinx: builds the claim table.
- Pixel: notices how rumors mutate.

## Location

- The Notice Wall and nearby Chronicle desk.

## Villain Or Conflict

- Public speed pressures the team to accept a story before the evidence is ready.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Public Mythology](../concepts/public-mythology.md) and [Trace Evidence](../concepts/trace-evidence.md).
- Defensive takeaway: repeated claims do not become verified by repetition.
- Teacher note: make the classification visual and story-driven.

## Real-World Inspiration

- Media amplification during famous hacker cases.

## Public Transformation Notes

- The Chronicle is a fictional student news desk, not a real publication.

## Safety Constraints

- Avoid real case claims not in the historical account.
- Emphasize verification and correction.

## Episode Hook

BL4CK4T drops four labels: `SEEN`, `GUESSED`, `REPEATED`, `UNKNOWN`.
