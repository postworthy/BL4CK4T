# The Phishmonger On Signal Row

## Summary

The Script Kitties investigate urgent glowing invitations appearing across Signal Row. The messages claim to be from BL4CK4T, but Jinx notices tiny inconsistencies, and the team learns to verify suspicious messages before acting.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the public article is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Jinx spots suspicious details and pushes the investigation.
- Cipher explains sender verification and trust signals.
- Pixel tests safe visual clues using copies of the message.
- Whiskers turns the findings into a team rule: pause, verify, report.

## Location

- [Signal Row](../locations/cybertropolis.md#signal-row), because the mission centers on public messages, urgency, and trusted-looking broadcasts.

## Villain Or Conflict

- [The Phishmonger](../villains/the-phishmonger.md) floods Signal Row with fake BL4CK4T invitations asking readers to confirm a secret passphrase.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Phishing](../concepts/phishing.md).
- Defensive takeaway: suspicious messages should be checked by sender, link destination, context, tone, and reporting path.
- Teacher note: emphasize verification habits without providing phishing templates.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Real phishing becomes fictional glowing invitations in Cybertropolis.
- Credentials become a harmless "hideout passphrase" that the team refuses to share.
- Reporting becomes alerting BL4CK4T and Signal Row's message wardens.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not include reusable phishing copy.
- Do not explain credential harvesting.
- Keep link inspection conceptual and safe.
- Emphasize asking for help and reporting.
