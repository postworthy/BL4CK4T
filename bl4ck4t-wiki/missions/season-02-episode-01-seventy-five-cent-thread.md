# Mission: The Seventy-Five Cent Thread

## Summary

The `-0.75` clue from the Hushline model reappears in the Ledger Lab's resource counter. Jinx notices first and refuses to let the number be dismissed as ordinary drift, but the team has to accept that a mismatch is only a starting point.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the Season 2 episode set is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Jinx: lead investigator who protects the small clue.
- Whiskers: keeps the team from turning suspicion into accusation.
- Pixel: wants the number to mean something immediately and learns to slow down.
- BL4CK4T: drops the season's first method clue.

## Location

- The Hideout: the Hushline model shows the first `-0.75`.
- Ledger Lab: the same value appears in a real shared-resource record.

## Villain Or Conflict

No adversary appears. The conflict is dismissal: a small record looks too minor to matter.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Anomaly Investigation](../concepts/anomaly-investigation.md) and [Shared-System Accounting](../concepts/shared-system-accounting.md).
- Defensive takeaway: small mismatches deserve preservation before interpretation.
- Teacher note: introduce the difference between a clue and a conclusion.

## Real-World Inspiration

- The Cuckoo's Egg accounting discrepancy, transformed through the Season 2 arc.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Real research accounting becomes the Ledger Lab resource counter.
- The historical discrepancy becomes `-0.75`, carried forward from Season 1.
- The story centers on defender observation, not intruder action.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not show how the mismatch was caused.
- Keep the episode focused on records, care, and restraint.

## Episode Hook

The Hushline model should have settled to zero. Instead, one red line remains: `-0.75`.
