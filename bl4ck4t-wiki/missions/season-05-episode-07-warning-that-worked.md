# Mission: The Warning That Worked

## Summary

Whiskers and Pixel help the Message Office write a city warning that tells people what to do without making them hide mistakes.

## Canon Status

Public canon once Season 5 publishes.

## Featured Characters

- Whiskers: leads public communication.
- Pixel: helps phrase the warning for curious readers.

## Location

- Message Office and Notice Wall.

## Villain Or Conflict

- Bad warnings can create silence, shame, and more spread.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Incident Reporting](../concepts/incident-reporting.md).
- Defensive takeaway: warnings should be clear, specific, and kind.
- Teacher note: make public communication part of defense.

## Real-World Inspiration

- User warning and help-desk response during email outbreaks.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Use city warning cards.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not include real indicators that act as instructions.
- Keep warning actions simple and defensive.

## Episode Hook

The first warning makes people hide; the second makes them report.
