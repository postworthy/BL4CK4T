# Season 7 Episode 1: The Red Mark

## Summary

The Season 6 red Service Map card reveals a deferred City Runtime repair notice with no decision owner.

## Featured Characters

- Pixel recognizes the common runtime sticker across ordinary city machines.
- Jinx treats the blank owner field as evidence.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Patch Debt](../concepts/patch-debt.md).
- Defensive takeaway: a known serious repair must have an owner and a visible decision.
- Teacher note: once a fix is known, silence becomes a risk decision.

## Episode Hook

One red card stays clipped to the Service Map after every flood-related card has cleared.

## Real-World Inspiration

- MS03-026 existed before Blaster became a public crisis.

## Public Transformation Notes

- The public story introduces the City Runtime as the BL4CK4T-world equivalent for widely deployed affected systems.

## Safety Constraints

- Avoid exploit mechanics.
- Emphasize inventory, ownership, and repair tracking.
