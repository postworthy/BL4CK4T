# Season 7 Episode 4: The Restarting Desks

## Summary

The City Runtime problem reaches ordinary desk terminals, causing lost work, frozen counters, support queues, and pressure for bounded emergency action.

## Featured Characters

- Cipher names uncertainty without freezing.
- Pixel sees harm through Milo's lost draft.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Emergency Change](../concepts/emergency-change.md) and [Desk Terminals](../concepts/desk-terminals.md).
- Defensive takeaway: endpoint instability can create real harm even when no records are stolen.
- Teacher note: maintenance protects people doing work, not only machines.

## Episode Hook

Milo's classroom desk terminal restarts before he can save the best paragraph of his report.

## Real-World Inspiration

- Blaster-era restarts, lost work, help-desk pressure, and emergency repair.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Real reboots become visible desk-terminal restarts in a clearly established Cybertropolis endpoint layer.

## Safety Constraints

- Avoid malware behavior.
- Emphasize consequence, scope, fallback, rollback, and prioritization.
