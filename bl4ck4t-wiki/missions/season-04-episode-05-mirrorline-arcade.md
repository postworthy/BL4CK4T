# Mission: Mirrorline Arcade

## Summary

The public rushes to Mirrorline Arcade after a Chronicle update, and the crowd makes the traces harder to read. Whiskers redirects the team from chase energy to preservation.

## Canon Status

Public canon once Season 4 publishes.

## Featured Characters

- Whiskers: protects people and process.
- Shadow: resists the urge to vanish into the crowd alone.

## Location

- Mirrorline Arcade.

## Villain Or Conflict

- The Caller benefits from public noise.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Trace Evidence](../concepts/trace-evidence.md) and evidence contamination.
- Defensive takeaway: crowds can destroy signal even with good intentions.
- Teacher note: make the consequence concrete through lost traces.

## Real-World Inspiration

- Public manhunt pressure and media-shaped pursuit.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Use crowd movement and reflections instead of real surveillance detail.

## Safety Constraints

- Avoid pursuit tactics.
- Emphasize careful reporting and staying out of the way.

## Episode Hook

The Arcade fills before the team can photograph the first route mark.
