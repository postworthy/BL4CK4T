# Mission: A Letter For Everybody

## Summary

Copies of the Glitter Letter appear from familiar senders, and Pixel learns that a trusted name can carry an unsafe message by accident.

## Canon Status

Public canon once Season 5 publishes.

## Featured Characters

- Pixel: feels the pull of curiosity and belonging.
- Shadow: notices the first messages arrived at impossible times.

## Location

- Letter Lane.

## Villain Or Conflict

- The Glitter Letter uses trust paths before anyone understands the spread.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Attachment Trust](../concepts/attachment-trust.md).
- Defensive takeaway: known sender does not equal expected message.
- Teacher note: avoid blaming senders who were affected.

## Real-World Inspiration

- ILOVEYOU spread through messages appearing to come from known contacts.
