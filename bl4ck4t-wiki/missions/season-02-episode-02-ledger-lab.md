# Mission: The Ledger Lab

## Summary

The Script Kitties learn how the Ledger Lab counts compute time, storage credits, project reservations, and print resources. Byte maps the normal noise so Jinx can tell whether `-0.75` is a stray pebble or the first marked stone in a path.

## Canon Status

Draft canon until the Season 2 episode set is approved.

## Featured Characters

- Jinx: studies normal records before naming a pattern.
- Byte: explains the Ledger Lab and sketches the first resource map.
- Pixel: discovers that accounting can be a sensor.

## Location

- Ledger Lab: old terminals, help counter, resource board, and project queue.

## Villain Or Conflict

The conflict is ambiguity. Most mismatches are harmless leftovers, which makes the repeated one harder to defend as important.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Logging And Audit Trails](../concepts/logging-and-audit-trails.md) and [Shared-System Accounting](../concepts/shared-system-accounting.md).
- Defensive takeaway: know normal behavior before calling something abnormal.
- Teacher note: show that "accounting" can mean resource use, not only money.

## Real-World Inspiration

- Research computing environments where shared usage records can expose unexpected behavior.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Historical lab usage becomes student project resources.
- Technical recordkeeping becomes visible through counters, queue slips, and status boards.

## Safety Constraints

- Do not provide attack mechanics.
- Keep examples abstract and fictional.

## Episode Hook

The Ledger Lab counter rolls through a dozen ordinary project charges, then stops on the same red decimal Jinx copied the night before.
