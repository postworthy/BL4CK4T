# Season 7 Episode 3: Red Door Fever

## Summary

Public-facing City Runtime systems begin repainting with red doors while service-room logs show the affected group is wider than one board.

## Featured Characters

- Jinx separates public symptoms from the underlying affected runtime.
- Shadow checks both the physical board and the service-room record.

## Lesson Payload

- Concept: [Patch Debt](../concepts/patch-debt.md) and [Service Rooms](../concepts/service-rooms.md).
- Defensive takeaway: public-facing systems need urgency because reachability changes risk.
- Teacher note: exposed systems are often where hidden maintenance debt becomes visible first.

## Episode Hook

A clinic board changes from `ROOM 4 READY` into a red door while the waiting room watches.

## Real-World Inspiration

- Early-2000s worms repeatedly showed the risk of exposed network services and delayed fixes.

## Public Transformation Notes

- Worm spread becomes visible public symptoms plus defender-side service evidence.

## Safety Constraints

- Avoid spread mechanics.
- Emphasize scope, isolation, and fallback paths.
