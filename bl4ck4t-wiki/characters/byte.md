# Byte

## Summary

Byte is the gadgeteer and builder. Byte uses terminals, simulations, and quick demos to make abstract concepts visible.

## Hero Anchor

Byte is anchored in the spirit of MacGyver: practical invention, improvisation, constraints, and non-destructive problem solving. Byte should be a younger maker who solves problems with scraps, patience, and safe prototypes.

## Current Traits

- Tool-oriented.
- Fast with terminals and devices.
- Interested in demos.
- Learns by building small working models.

## Voice

Byte speaks in practical build steps and quick status checks. Byte often wants to spin up a toy model or simulator.

## Strengths

- Makes concepts runnable.
- Builds lab-safe versions of complicated systems.
- Translates theory into tools.

## Flaws

- May trust tool output before checking the model.
- Can over-focus on the gadget instead of the learner.

## Visual Motifs And Tools

- Tablet.
- Old terminal.
- Cable bundles.
- Simulators and dashboards.

## Lesson Role

Use Byte for safe tooling, demos, toy crypto, network labs, and validation checks.

## Backstory Seeds

- A salvaged device collection in the hideout.
- A favorite broken machine that Byte keeps trying to revive.

## Growth Direction

Byte should grow from gadget-first builder into thoughtful engineer. The backstory should center on repairing discarded tech in The Old Stack and learning that a clever tool still needs verification, documentation, and care for the people using it.
