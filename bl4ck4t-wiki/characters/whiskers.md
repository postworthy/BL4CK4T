# Whiskers

## Summary

Whiskers is the Script Kitties' field leader. He helps the team frame the problem, keeps the mission moving, and often states the shared lesson.

## Hero Anchor

Whiskers is anchored in the spirit of Captain America / Steve Rogers: principled leadership, responsibility, moral courage, and team-first action. This should guide Whiskers as a younger character learning that leadership means listening before commanding.

## Current Traits

- Leadership.
- Synthesis.
- Trust and teamwork framing.
- Calm mission focus.

## Voice

Whiskers speaks in steady, direct lines. He often turns scattered team discoveries into a single action plan or moral.

## Strengths

- Keeps the team moving.
- Notices when a mission needs coordination.
- Makes the final lesson feel shared.

## Flaws

- Can step into leadership too quickly before quieter teammates have spoken.
- Sometimes wants a clean answer before the situation is fully understood.

## Visual Motifs And Tools

- Field notebook.
- Rooftop vantage points.
- Signal markers and team checklists.

## Lesson Role

Use Whiskers to frame consent, teamwork, and the final defensive takeaway.

## Backstory Seeds

- Why the team trusts Whiskers.
- What leadership mistake taught Whiskers to listen before acting.

## Growth Direction

Whiskers should grow from eager field leader into a steadier moral center. His formative backstory should involve a small leadership mistake where rushing to decide caused confusion, teaching him that the strongest leader makes space for the whole team.

## Published Season Canon

- Season 1: keeps the team focused when the Echo Grid mystery turns into public consequence.
- Season 2: bridges the team to the Glass Bureau and protects the Ledger Lab case from overstatement.
- Season 3: keeps Byte's accountability useful rather than shame-driven and keeps affected students centered.

## Future Use Notes

Whiskers should lead by making space for evidence, repair, and the right teammate's expertise instead of forcing a clean answer early.
