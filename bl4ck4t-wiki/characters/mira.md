# Mira

## Summary

Mira is the Signal Row repair figure who helps establish the Hushline and turns the Echo Grid lesson into architectural repair.

## Hero Anchor

Mira is anchored in the spirit of the seasoned civic engineer: practical, local, and more interested in working fixes than spectacle.

## Current Traits

- Repair-minded.
- Local to Signal Row.
- Direct.
- Protective of public infrastructure.

## Voice

Mira speaks in repair terms: pairs, routes, messages, and what must be separated before the city can trust a path.

## Strengths

- Understands Signal Row infrastructure.
- Helps translate the team's model into repair.
- Knows when a clever explanation must become a working fix.

## Flaws

- May have little patience for rumor culture.
- Can underestimate how much young learners need to understand before helping.

## Visual Motifs And Tools

- Repair tags.
- Silver labels.
- Signal diagrams.
- Work lights.

## Lesson Role

Use Mira when a story needs infrastructure repair, local Signal Row context, or an adult ally who cares about architecture.

## Backstory Seeds

- Has maintained Signal Row systems through multiple generations of upgrades.
- May know old Echo Grid history that the team has not yet heard.

## Growth Direction

Mira can become the recurring Signal Row repair ally, but should remain grounded in work rather than mystery.

## Published Season Canon

- Season 1: helps establish the Hushline, the fix that separates Signal Row messages from routing commands.

## Future Use Notes

Mira should preserve the Hushline as a successful repair. Future Signal Row issues should be new problems, not a simple failure of her fix.
