# Rook

## Summary

Rook is the most visible Row Rebels figure in Season 1. Rook carries curiosity, status-seeking, protest energy, and disruption around the Echo Grid without becoming a flat villain.

## Hero Anchor

Rook is anchored in the spirit of the charismatic rebel: sharp, performative, and capable of growth if someone separates the pose from the person.

## Current Traits

- Curious.
- Status-aware.
- Public-facing.
- Prone to spectacle.

## Voice

Rook speaks like someone performing for a crowd, but the best lines should hint that Rook also cares what the city is becoming.

## Strengths

- Understands Signal Row rumor culture.
- Can move a crowd.
- Notices when institutions ignore young people.

## Flaws

- Treats visibility as proof.
- Can confuse disruption with impact.

## Visual Motifs And Tools

- Crowned marks.
- Row Rebels stickers.
- Platform gatherings.
- Loud public gestures.

## Lesson Role

Use Rook to explore mixed motives: curiosity, protest, status, and harm in the same person.

## Backstory Seeds

- Has a personal reason to distrust official Signal Row explanations.
- May have learned old system lore from older kids rather than caretakers.

## Growth Direction

Rook should remain redeemable but not sanitized. Future appearances should preserve the tension between insight and recklessness.

## Published Season Canon

- Season 1: appears through Row Rebels activity, crowned marks, Crunch Charm culture, and the public pressure around the Echo Grid.

## Future Use Notes

Do not retcon Rook into the sole cause of Season 1. Rook is a prominent participant in a mixed-motive culture.
