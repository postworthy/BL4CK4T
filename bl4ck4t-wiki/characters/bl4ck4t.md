# BL4CK4T

## Summary

BL4CK4T is the unseen mentor behind the drops that guide the Script Kitties. BL4CK4T appears through signs, encrypted messages, pawprint marks, and city-scale learning environments rather than direct presence.

## Hero Anchor

BL4CK4T is anchored in the spirit of Gandalf: mysterious mentor, indirect guidance, challenge-based growth, and benevolent restraint. BL4CK4T should guide through drops and tests rather than direct answers, allowing the Script Kitties to become capable on their own.

## Current Canon

- Sends missions as "drops."
- Uses a pawprint mark.
- Speaks in concise, lesson-driven messages.
- Teaches through challenge, not lecture.
- Frames technical learning as character growth.

## Operating Limits

- BL4CK4T can stage safe puzzles, simulations, and clues.
- BL4CK4T should not solve the mission directly.
- BL4CK4T should not ask the Script Kitties to attack real systems.
- BL4CK4T's messages should point toward curiosity, ethics, and defense.

## Voice

BL4CK4T speaks in compact, memorable lines. The voice should feel mysterious but not cruel.

## Story Use

Use BL4CK4T to start the mission, raise the right question, and close the story with the lesson's deeper meaning.

## Open Questions

- Is BL4CK4T a person, collective, AI, legend, or something else?
- Why did BL4CK4T choose the Script Kitties?
- What constraints prevent BL4CK4T from appearing directly?

## Growth Direction

BL4CK4T should remain publicly mysterious while privately serving as the story's challenge architect. Future canon can reveal pieces of origin slowly, but every reveal should preserve the mentor's core function: preparing younger learners to think ethically without doing the thinking for them.

## Published Season Canon

- Uses compact drops to start, redirect, or close lessons.
- Does not solve the team's cases directly.
- Repeatedly asks the team to listen, count, map, preserve, stop, and reflect.
- Season 3 confirms BL4CK4T can guide response discipline without becoming the responder.

## Future Use Notes

BL4CK4T drops should continue to name the right question, not give the full answer.
