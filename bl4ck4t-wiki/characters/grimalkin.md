# Grimalkin

## Summary

Grimalkin is the planner. He recognizes patterns, thinks ahead, and values early preparation.

## Hero Anchor

Grimalkin is anchored in the spirit of Batman / Bruce Wayne: preparation, threat modeling, discipline, caution, and adaptive planning. The anchor should be softened for BL4CK4T; Grimalkin is a younger planner learning that preparation must leave room for trust and experimentation.

## Current Traits

- Strategic.
- Pattern-aware.
- Risk-conscious.
- Future-oriented.

## Voice

Grimalkin speaks with measured caution. He likes timelines, probabilities, and quiet warnings.

## Strengths

- Plans ahead.
- Spots weak assumptions.
- Connects today's lesson to future consequences.

## Flaws

- Can over-plan when a small experiment would teach faster.
- May sound gloomy when he is trying to be practical.

## Visual Motifs And Tools

- City maps.
- Timeline strings.
- Threat-model cards.

## Lesson Role

Use Grimalkin for risk modeling, preparation, patching, backups, and long-term thinking.

## Backstory Seeds

- A past close call that made planning important.
- A personal project involving maps, timelines, or threat models.

## Growth Direction

Grimalkin should grow from over-planner into resilient strategist. His backstory should include a contained close call where a missing plan created trouble, followed by later stories where he learns that no plan survives without teamwork.
