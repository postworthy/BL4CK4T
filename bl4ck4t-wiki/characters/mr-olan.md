# Mr. Olan

## Summary

Mr. Olan is the librarian connected to the Season 2 library terminal thread. He preserves public summaries after the Glass Bureau case stub arrives.

## Hero Anchor

Mr. Olan is anchored in the spirit of the careful archivist: skeptical, helpful, and protective of public records.

## Current Traits

- Observant.
- Skeptical of rushed conclusions.
- Protective of library systems.
- Helpful when process is followed.

## Voice

Mr. Olan should sound precise and dry. He respects records more than excitement.

## Strengths

- Understands library terminal context.
- Protects public summaries.
- Adds institutional memory beyond the Ledger Lab.

## Flaws

- Suspicious of outsiders arriving with incomplete theories.
- May over-protect local records until ownership is clear.

## Visual Motifs And Tools

- Public summary slips.
- Library terminals.
- Archive desk.
- Quiet status lights.

## Visual Description For Art

Mr. Olan should look like a careful civic-record librarian: precise, dry, and protective of public summaries. His visual world is the library terminal, archive desk, rain-garden window, public summary slips, and quiet status lights.

Render him with neat stacks of public slips, a terminal screen angled away from casual view, and a posture that suggests he is deciding whether the visitors have brought enough process to deserve access. Clothing should be tidy and understated, with small archive or library details rather than dramatic authority cues.

Avoid using him as a generic elder or city official. His visual identity should stay local to library records and public-terminal stewardship.

## Lesson Role

Use Mr. Olan for public records, library systems, and the value of local caretakers in cross-system incidents.

## Backstory Seeds

- Has maintained public terminal records through many student projects.
- Knows old Civic Learning Grid habits that no single lab remembers.

## Growth Direction

Mr. Olan can become a recurring civic-record ally if future stories involve libraries, public terminals, or old trust paths.

## Published Season Canon

- Season 2: preserves the public summary at the library terminal and watches Grimalkin's map with interest and suspicion.

## Future Use Notes

Mr. Olan should remain local and specific. Do not use him as a generic city authority.
