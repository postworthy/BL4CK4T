# Ms. Vale

## Summary

Ms. Vale is the Ledger Lab caretaker who helps the Script Kitties preserve records, protect students, and turn strange incidents into useful reports.

## Hero Anchor

Ms. Vale is anchored in the spirit of the practical mentor: part librarian, part lab manager, part incident coordinator. She should feel steady, tired, precise, and protective.

## Current Traits

- Careful with records.
- Protective of students.
- Process-aware.
- Willing to help when evidence is clear.

## Voice

Ms. Vale speaks plainly. She prefers forms, records, and useful truth over drama.

## Strengths

- Knows the Ledger Lab and Civic Learning Grid.
- Protects affected students.
- Turns team discoveries into caretaker-actionable work.

## Flaws

- Can appear slow because she refuses to act on weak evidence.
- May trust process before she trusts a hunch.

## Visual Motifs And Tools

- Tea mug.
- Help counter.
- Red folders.
- Archive sleeves.
- Response forms.

## Lesson Role

Use Ms. Vale for evidence handling, caretaker review, student protection, and practical response procedure.

## Backstory Seeds

- Long history maintaining student lab systems.
- Has seen small mistakes become large cleanup jobs.

## Growth Direction

Ms. Vale should grow into a recurring adult ally who respects the Script Kitties because they bring better records over time.

## Published Season Canon

- Season 2: helps preserve Ledger Lab records, protects Project Orchard, works with the Glass Bureau, and gives Jinx the archived `-0.75` case copy.
- Season 3: reviews Byte's sandbox plan, opens The Old Stack context, receives the Builder's Note, and helps establish the First Bell Desk.

## Future Use Notes

Ms. Vale should not become a shortcut authority who solves cases. She should ask for useful evidence and make the team's work safer.
