# Shadow

## Summary

Shadow is the quiet observer. Shadow notices authenticity, subtle changes, and the power of small actions carried over time.

## Hero Anchor

Shadow is anchored in the spirit of T'Challa / Black Panther: quiet discipline, observation, principled restraint, and protective competence. Shadow should be a younger protector whose strength is patience, presence, and careful action.

## Current Traits

- Quiet.
- Observant.
- Stealth-minded.
- Attentive to hidden details.

## Voice

Shadow uses short lines. When Shadow speaks, the detail should matter.

## Strengths

- Notices subtle inconsistencies.
- Watches patterns over time.
- Understands quiet persistence.

## Flaws

- May hold back an observation too long.
- Can be hard for the team to read.

## Visual Motifs And Tools

- Low light.
- Reflections.
- Small marks.
- Hidden paths and quiet sensors.

## Lesson Role

Use Shadow for authenticity clues, stealthy observation, small beginnings, and hidden signals.

## Backstory Seeds

- A reason Shadow stays near the edge of the room.
- A personal lesson about small beginnings.

## Growth Direction

Shadow should grow from silent watcher into trusted protector. The backstory should involve a quiet act of noticing that kept someone safe, teaching Shadow that small observations can carry real responsibility.

## Published Season Canon

- Season 1: notices rooftop beacon behavior and sticker-path evidence around the Echo Grid.
- Season 2: spots physical evidence such as terminal warmth, moved panels, and relay state.
- Season 3: checks room lights, fans, and physical room state during containment and recovery.

## Future Use Notes

Shadow's observations should continue to matter materially. Avoid using Shadow only as atmosphere.
