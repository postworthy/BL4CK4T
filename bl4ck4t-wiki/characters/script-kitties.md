# The Script Kitties

## Summary

The Script Kitties are the learner crew at the center of BL4CK4T stories. They solve drops together, each bringing a different learning style and personality to the mission.

## Members

- [Whiskers](whiskers.md) - field leader and synthesizer.
- [Grimalkin](grimalkin.md) - planner and pattern recognizer.
- [Pixel](pixel.md) - curious experimenter and analogy maker.
- [Byte](byte.md) - gadgeteer, terminal user, and builder.
- [Cipher](cipher.md) - codebreaker and math explainer.
- [Jinx](jinx.md) - investigator and risk spotter.
- [Shadow](shadow.md) - quiet observer and stealth-minded learner.

## Team Pattern

The team learns by splitting a problem into perspectives, testing ideas, comparing results, and turning the solution into a shared lesson.

## Team Visual Direction

The Script Kitties should read as a learner crew, not a uniformed squad. The character-card assets establish hoodie color as the fastest visual recognizer: Whiskers bright green, Grimalkin olive-green, Pixel bright orange, Byte cyan-blue, Cipher purple, Jinx magenta, and Shadow teal-green. Most of the crew share dark charcoal or black cat silhouettes, pointed ears, pink noses, thick outlines, and bright eyes; the contrast should come from posture, props, hoodie color, and role-specific visual language.

Each member should keep a distinct silhouette tied to role: Whiskers with field notes and squared leadership posture, Grimalkin with maps, planning cards, and situational glasses, Pixel with a compact energetic silhouette and hands-on prototypes, Byte with a cyan-blue hooded layer and tablet, Cipher with a purple hoodie and clean diagrams, Jinx with magenta hoodie, case notes, and warning tags, and Shadow with teal-green hooded edge-of-room observation.

Group art should keep Cybertropolis visible through neon signs, rooftops, old terminals, civic boards, maps, stickers, and pawprint drops. The crowd image supports dense city scenes full of other cat citizens, glowing signs, reflective pavement, and varied hoodie colors around the core team. Avoid giving every member identical gear; the team's visual strength is collaborative contrast.

## Group Voice

The team should sound collaborative. They disagree by testing, asking, and comparing, not by belittling each other.

## Common Mission Roles

- Whiskers keeps the mission coordinated.
- Grimalkin asks what might happen later.
- Pixel creates concrete experiments and analogies.
- Byte builds safe demos.
- Cipher explains the formal idea.
- Jinx investigates suspicious details.
- Shadow notices what others miss.

## Public Safety Boundary

The Script Kitties do not break into real systems. Their missions happen in fictional city systems, safe labs, simulations, or defensive investigations.
