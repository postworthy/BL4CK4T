# Season 7: The Patch Bell War Arc

## Import Summary

Season 7 transforms the MSBlaster/Welchia documentary spine into a Cybertropolis maintenance crisis. The red Service Map mark from Season 6 leads to the Civic Pages District, where a known repair was delayed because the signs still worked. Red Door fever spreads across public pages. The city builds a trusted Repair Lane, then faces the Helpful Ghost, a silver unauthorized repair force that fixes some symptoms while breaking consent.

## Season Shape

1. The red mark from Season 6 points to a known unrepaired Civic Pages system.
2. The Red Clerk explains that the signs still worked and old repairs once broke public boards.
3. Red Door fever appears across public pages.
4. The harm becomes ordinary: lost drafts, rebooting desks, stale notices, and confused citizens.
5. Cipher hesitates because no option is perfectly safe.
6. Byte builds repair tools and initially admires the Helpful Ghost's effectiveness.
7. Jinx insists that repair must have a source, signature, and ledger trail.
8. The city rejects both silent delay and forced repair.
9. The Patch Bell becomes durable practice.
10. A quiet pre-fever access log shows someone read source plans before the loud crisis, opening Season 8.

## Character Arcs

- Cipher learns that responsible defenders sometimes act under bounded evidence rather than waiting for impossible certainty.
- Byte learns that a clever fix without authorization is still a violation.
- Jinx keeps the repair process honest by demanding source, signature, and consent.
- Whiskers helps the city explain why patching is not private housekeeping when public services depend on it.
- Grimalkin makes rollback and risk acceptance visible.
- Pixel sees that a board that "still works" can still be carrying risk.
- Shadow notices when the pages look repaired but the rooms still behave wrong.

## Season 8 Bridge

After the Patch Bell is adopted, Shadow and Jinx find a quiet access log from before the Red Door fever. Someone read source plans from a protected room and left almost no disturbance. The next season should treat this as a quiet-intrusion bridge, not as another worm.
