# Season 7: The Patch Bell War Arc

## Historical Import

The historical documentary movement becomes a Cybertropolis story about patch, isolate, rollback, and consent-aware emergency repair. The season keeps the high-level flow of symptom, system, response, and lasting change while replacing historical specifics with fictional districts, artifacts, and adversaries.

## Season Promise

Season 6 leaves one red service marked as old and unpatched. Cipher leads the emotional arc. The season teaches that A known flaw ignored long enough becomes an emergency everyone inherits.

## Core Canon

- Main artifact: [Patch Bell](../artifacts/patch-bell.md).
- Main location: [Civic Pages District](../locations/civic-pages-district.md).
- Main faction: [Repair Lane](../factions/repair-lane.md).
- Main adversary: [The Red Clerk](../villains/red-clerk.md).
- Main concepts: [Patch Debt](../concepts/patch-debt.md), [Verified Repair](../concepts/verified-repair.md), and [Emergency Change](../concepts/emergency-change.md).

## Character Movement

Cipher's strength becomes pressure. The case forces growth through patch, isolate, rollback, and consent-aware emergency repair. Byte supports the arc and carries the practical investigation.

## Episode Arc

1. The Red Mark - The unpatched service leads the team into the Civic Pages District.
2. Still Working - The Red Clerk argues that old signs should be left alone because they still glow.
3. Red Door Fever - Public pages repaint themselves and Cipher hesitates over incomplete evidence.
4. Restart Rain - Desks restart and one student loses work before saving.
5. The Repair Lane - Jinx and Whiskers build a trusted path for verified updates.
6. The Helpful Ghost - A silver repairer begins fixing systems without permission.
7. It Worked - Byte admires the clever repair until he sees the boundary it crossed.
8. Patch Or Isolate - Cipher learns to act under bounded evidence and record the risk.
9. The Bell Rings - The Patch Bell becomes civic practice and the quiet source-plan clue appears.

## Cliffhanger

A quiet log shows source plans were read before the loud repair crisis.
