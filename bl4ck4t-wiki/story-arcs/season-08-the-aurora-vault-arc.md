# Season 8: The Aurora Vault Arc

## Historical Import

The historical documentary movement becomes a Cybertropolis story about crown-jewel protection, access review, and quiet anomaly reading. The season keeps the high-level flow of symptom, system, response, and lasting change while replacing historical specifics with fictional districts, artifacts, and adversaries.

## Season Promise

A log shows that source plans were read before the Patch Bell War. Jinx leads the emotional arc. The season teaches that A copy can be dangerous when it reveals how tomorrow is protected.

## Core Canon

- Main artifact: [Crown Jewel Register](../artifacts/crown-jewel-register.md).
- Main location: [Aurora Vault](../locations/aurora-vault.md).
- Main faction: [Aurora Caretakers](../factions/aurora-caretakers.md).
- Main adversary: [The Glass Dragon](../villains/glass-dragon.md).
- Main concepts: [Crown Jewel Assets](../concepts/crown-jewel-assets.md), [Access Boundaries](../concepts/access-boundaries.md), and [Quiet Persistence](../concepts/quiet-persistence.md).

## Character Movement

Jinx's strength becomes pressure. The case forces growth through crown-jewel protection, access review, and quiet anomaly reading. Grimalkin supports the arc and carries the practical investigation.

## Episode Arc

1. The Quiet Log - Jinx finds a source-plan read that happened before the loud crisis.
2. Dust On The Shelf - A blueprint sits in place, yet the shelf tells Grimalkin it was touched.
3. The Wrong Key - A junior key opens a room it should never reach.
4. The Register - The Crown Jewel Register teaches the team that not all secrets carry equal risk.
5. Mirror Hall - Trusted access appears in the wrong pattern at the wrong hour.
6. Nothing Happened - Pixel struggles with a theft where every object remains visible.
7. Old Vault Rules - Grimalkin admits that old wisdom must be renewed.
8. Glass Scales - A limited villain-view episode shows the Glass Dragon choosing patience.
9. The Partial Blueprint - The team rotates keys, but one copied plan points to a machine below.

## Cliffhanger

A copied blueprint points beneath the city toward an old physical machine.
