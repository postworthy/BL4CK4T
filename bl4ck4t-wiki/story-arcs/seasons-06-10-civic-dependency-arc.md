# Seasons 6-10: The Civic Dependency Arc

## Planning Status

This is a macro planning document, not final episode canon. It defines the intended five-season direction after Season 5 while preserving the source-first workflow: each season still requires a verified historical account before documentary treatment, BL4CK4T-world import, production bible, mission packets, drafts, and publication.

## Arc Thesis

Cybertropolis learns to become honest about dependence.

The first five main seasons establish the Script Kitties as clever young investigators. Seasons 6-10 should mature them into civic defenders who understand that security is not only stopping villains. It is caring for a city that depends on fragile services, maintenance, trust boundaries, physical machines, and ordinary devices.

## Creative Rule

Do not make these seasons only bigger. Make them deeper.

Each season should keep the scale human even as the world expands. A clinic intake board spinning forever, a lost draft after a reboot, a copied blueprint, a lying gauge, or a toy with a default name can carry more BL4CK4T weight than a generic citywide catastrophe.

## Recurring Civic Artifacts

- Season 6: Service Map - shows what depends on what.
- Season 7: Patch Bell - forces known flaws to be patched, isolated, or publicly risk-accepted.
- Season 8: Crown Jewel Register - identifies assets that protect the city's future.
- Season 9: Truth Gauge - compares system claims, independent sensing, and physical reality.
- Season 10: Device Census - counts and classifies connected devices.

## Lesson Deepening

- Season 6 deepens Season 5: harm can occur when nothing is stolen and everything stops.
- Season 7 deepens Season 3: responsibility does not end after launch.
- Season 8 deepens Season 2: small evidence matters most when the attacker is patient.
- Season 9 deepens Season 1: a system that controls the world must not be the only witness to itself.
- Season 10 deepens Season 6: every connected thing joins the civic body.

## Structural Variation

The standard BL4CK4T article rhythm should remain recognizable, but each season may vary structure once or twice:

- Cold open episode: begin inside consequence before cause is understood.
- No-drop episode: the Script Kitties act from learned responsibility without a new BL4CK4T prompt.
- Villain-view episode: show limited adversary choices without operational detail.
- Witness episode: center an ordinary citizen, student, caretaker, teacher, or shop owner.
- Aftermath episode: linger on repair, apology, policy, and lingering effects after the technical response.

## Larger Lore, Slowly

Use larger mythology only where it serves the lesson:

- Mirror Cities for foreign intrusion, shared tools, copied blueprints, and diplomatic tension.
- Old Maintenance Guilds for legacy upkeep, fragmented adult institutions, and Grimalkin backstory.
- Nameplate Factory for default names, shipped risk, and manufacturer responsibility.
- First Builders for old physical systems and historical city design.
- Adversary Gallery as a cumulative BL4CK4T archive of case symbols.

## End State

By the end of Season 10, the Script Kitties should understand that cybersecurity is woven into food, health, learning, memory, communication, homes, and public life. That prepares the next macro arc for ransomware, supply-chain compromise, cloud trust, identity abuse, deepfakes, AI deception, platform manipulation, and insider risk.
