# Origin Arc

## Summary

The origin arc establishes BL4CK4T's drops, the Script Kitties' team dynamics, and the idea that each mission teaches a real concept.

## Current Episodes

1. The Hidden Code - first encrypted drop and Caesar cipher.
2. Keys To The City - public/private cryptography and shared secrets.
3. The Interest Of Time - compound interest and long-term choices.

## Arc Direction

The next stories can deepen character backstories, introduce recurring adversaries, and connect ClassOverride labs to story missions.

## Season Relationship

This arc currently functions as the [Pilot Season](../seasons/pilot-season.md). It may remain standalone, or later stories may reinterpret it as BL4CK4T's first calibration test for the Script Kitties.
