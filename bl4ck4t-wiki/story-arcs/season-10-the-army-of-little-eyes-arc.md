# Season 10: The Army Of Little Eyes Arc

## Historical Import

The historical documentary movement becomes a Cybertropolis story about inventory, safer defaults, education, and manufacturer responsibility. The season keeps the high-level flow of symptom, system, response, and lasting change while replacing historical specifics with fictional districts, artifacts, and adversaries.

## Season Promise

Many tiny devices answer under repeated factory names. Pixel leads the emotional arc. The season teaches that Forgotten devices can still answer someone else.

## Core Canon

- Main artifact: [Device Census](../artifacts/device-census.md).
- Main location: [Little Things District](../locations/little-things-district.md).
- Main faction: [Nameplate Factory](../factions/nameplate-factory.md).
- Main adversary: [The Shepherd Of Little Eyes](../villains/shepherd-of-little-eyes.md).
- Main concepts: [IoT Devices](../concepts/iot-devices.md), [Device Inventory](../concepts/device-inventory.md), and [Default Credentials](../concepts/default-credentials.md).

## Character Movement

Pixel's strength becomes pressure. The case forces growth through inventory, safer defaults, education, and manufacturer responsibility. Jinx supports the arc and carries the practical investigation.

## Episode Arc

1. The Toy Owl Blinks - Pixel sees a toy answer a call nobody in the room made.
2. Ordinary Doors - Traffic comes through homes, classrooms, shops, and closets.
3. Please Do Not Blame Them - Whiskers stops the city from shaming device owners.
4. The Device Census - Jinx begins counting every connected thing that can answer.
5. Factory Names - The team finds repeated names and default keys across many devices.
6. Nameplate Factory - Byte asks why so many devices shipped with the same weak habits.
7. The Shepherd Calls - The Shepherd gathers devices he does not own.
8. Caretaker Checklist - Pixel teaches owners to rename, update, isolate, or retire devices.
9. Better Names - A toy gets a safer name, and the final clue points at the maker.

## Cliffhanger

The repeated names trace back to a shared update supplier.

All named recurring season entities are represented in the wiki. Incidental citizens, clerks, and students can remain texture unless future stories reuse them.
