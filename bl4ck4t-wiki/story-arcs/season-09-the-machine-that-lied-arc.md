# Season 9: The Machine That Lied Arc

## Historical Import

The historical documentary movement becomes a Cybertropolis story about independent sensing, manual checks, and safety-first response. The season keeps the high-level flow of symptom, system, response, and lasting change while replacing historical specifics with fictional districts, artifacts, and adversaries.

## Season Promise

The partial blueprint points into the machines beneath Cybertropolis. Shadow leads the emotional arc. The season teaches that A screen is only one witness when machines can affect the physical world.

## Core Canon

- Main artifact: [Truth Gauge](../artifacts/truth-gauge.md).
- Main location: [Foundry Quarter](../locations/foundry-quarter.md).
- Main faction: [Foundry Caretakers](../factions/foundry-caretakers.md).
- Main adversary: [The False Engineer](../villains/false-engineer.md).
- Main concepts: [Telemetry Integrity](../concepts/telemetry-integrity.md), [Independent Sensing](../concepts/independent-sensing.md), and [Cyber-Physical Safety](../concepts/cyber-physical-safety.md).

## Character Movement

Shadow's strength becomes pressure. The case forces growth through independent sensing, manual checks, and safety-first response. Byte supports the arc and carries the practical investigation.

## Episode Arc

1. Normal Green - Panels glow green while pipes shudder in the Foundry Quarter.
2. The Floor Said Run - Shadow hears vibration that the dashboard refuses to show.
3. Manual Room - Old diagrams and caretaker notes become evidence.
4. The Truth Gauge - The team compares screen claims, independent sensors, and physical signs.
5. Elegant Harm - Byte sees the cleverness and then sees the danger it hides.
6. Safety Line - Grimalkin marks the line where cleverness must stop.
7. False Readings - The False Engineer makes instruments disagree with one another.
8. Inspection Walk - Caretakers and Script Kitties restore manual routines.
9. Little Eyes - The machines recover, and Pixel spots many small watchers with matching names.

## Cliffhanger

Pixel notices tiny devices answering under repeated factory names.
