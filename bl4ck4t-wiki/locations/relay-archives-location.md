# Relay Archives Location

## Summary

Relay Archives is the archive-adjacent location where old research relay infrastructure sits behind locked cabinets and caretaker controls.

## Story Use

Use this location when a story needs old infrastructure that remains connected enough to matter.

## Visual Motifs

- Locked relay cabinets.
- Metal grilles.
- Archive annex halls.
- Status lights.
- Maintenance panels.

## Canon Notes

- Season 2 uses the old research relay as the third point after Ledger Lab and library.
- The location is maintained by the Relay Archives faction.
