# Packet Market

## Summary

Packet Market is a maker-and-rumor district where stickers, zines, charms, tools, and partial explanations move quickly.

## Story Use

Use Packet Market for supply-chain lessons, rumor spread, provenance, public tool culture, and unsafe simplification of complicated ideas.

## Visual Motifs

- Maker stalls.
- Zines and stickers.
- Tool crates.
- Charm displays.
- Package carts.

## Canon Notes

- The Little Blue Pawprint spreads through Packet Market.
- The Crunch Charm becomes public myth here.
- Future tool stories should treat Packet Market as lively but provenance-sensitive.
