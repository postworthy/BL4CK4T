# Queue District

## Summary

The Queue District is the Cybertropolis civic-services district where public requests are sorted, prioritized, delayed, answered, or handed to fallback desks.

## Story Use

Use the Queue District for Season 6 scenes about service availability: clinic intake boards, school request lines, public notices, appointment kiosks, news mirrors, and service windows that must keep answering under pressure.

## Visual Motifs

- Number slips.
- Request baskets.
- Service windows.
- Lamps for `working`, `degraded`, `unavailable`, and `unknown`.

## Canon Notes

- The Queue District connects to the Message Office, Status Wall, and Service Bell Tower.
- It is public infrastructure, not a villain lair.
- It should show both systems and people waiting on them.
