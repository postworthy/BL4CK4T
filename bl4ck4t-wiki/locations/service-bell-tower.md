# Service Bell Tower

## Summary

The Service Bell Tower is the Cybertropolis signal point that rings when a civic service becomes unreachable or severely degraded.

## Story Use

Use the tower sparingly. It should mark the moment when a service problem becomes civic, not every small delay.

## Visual Motifs

- Bells with different tones for degraded and unavailable.
- A tower ledger recording when the bell rang and when service recovered.
- Echoes that travel toward Queue District and the Status Wall.

## Canon Notes

- The Service Bell Tower is a response signal, not a panic device.
- It should work with the Status Wall and Service Map.
- It can recur in later infrastructure seasons.
