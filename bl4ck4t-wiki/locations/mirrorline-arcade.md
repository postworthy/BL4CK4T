# Mirrorline Arcade

## Summary

Mirrorline Arcade is a public passage in Cybertropolis filled with reflection glass, message kiosks, practice terminals, and old payphone booths. Season 4 uses it for identity confusion and trace evidence.

## Story Use

The Arcade lets the team show how reflections, echoes, and copied voices can mislead without proving who is behind a message.

## Visual Motifs

- Reflection glass.
- Payphone booths.
- Kiosk light strips.
- Floor arrows that double back.

## Canon Notes

- Mirrorline Arcade connects naturally to Keylight Gate and Signal Row.
- It should remain a public place, not a secret villain lair.
- Shadow's physical observation skills fit this location.
