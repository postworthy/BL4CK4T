# Keylight Gate

## Summary

Keylight Gate is the Cybertropolis district for locks, keys, signatures, certificates, and identity metaphors.

## Story Use

Use Keylight Gate for cryptography, authentication, authorization, and trust lessons.

## Visual Motifs

- Public locks.
- Private key vaults.
- Sealed messages.
- Glowing gates.
- Signature stamps.

## Canon Notes

- The district is established in the world bible as a cryptography metaphor space.
- Future cryptography stories should use it when a physical trust setting helps the lesson.
