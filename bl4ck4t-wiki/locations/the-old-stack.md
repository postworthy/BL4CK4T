# The Old Stack

## Summary

The Old Stack is a cluster of retired learning rooms beneath the Ledger Lab. It still connects to old classroom simulators through maintenance paths that most students never notice.

## Story Use

- The Copycat Sprite reaches The Old Stack after a sandbox boundary fails.
- Shadow finds timing clues in its maintenance lights.
- Grimalkin uses it as the first containment map anchor.

## Visual Motifs

- Dust covers.
- Retired terminals.
- Warm panels.
- Old room lights and low fans.
- Maintenance stairs under newer labs.

## Canon Notes

The Old Stack should not contradict the Hushline or the Glass Bureau. It is old lab infrastructure, not Signal Row's Echo Grid.
