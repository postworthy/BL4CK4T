# Civic Pages District

## Summary

The Civic Pages District maintains public signs, school boards, weather panels, permit kiosks, library notices, clinic displays, and other city-facing information pages.

## Story Use

- Makes patch debt visible through familiar public surfaces.
- Connects Season 6's red Service Map mark to a specific old public display system.
- Gives the Red Clerk a believable home: a district that values stability because citizens depend on visible signs.

## Visual Motifs

Rows of public boards, old display frames, paper backup notices, repair ladders, and dim red door icons beneath otherwise normal pages.

## Canon Notes

The district should not replace the Message Office, Status Wall, or Queue District. It handles public display and civic page infrastructure.
