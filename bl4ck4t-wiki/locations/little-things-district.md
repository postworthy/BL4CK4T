# Little Things District

## Summary

Little Things District is the main Season 10 setting for The Army Of Little Eyes.

## Story Use

Use this location for scenes where the civic lesson becomes concrete through people waiting, repairing, mapping, checking, or caring for systems.

## Visual Motifs

- Public desks, labels, lamps, maps, gauges, or device tags.
- A city-scale problem shown through small human details.

## Canon Notes

This location enters canon during Season 10. Later seasons may reference it as part of Cybertropolis civic infrastructure.
