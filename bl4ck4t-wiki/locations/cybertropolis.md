# Cybertropolis

## Summary

Cybertropolis is the neon city where BL4CK4T stories unfold. It works as both a place and a metaphor: networks become streets, billboards become message buses, locks become cryptographic systems, and city hazards become classroom-safe cybersecurity lessons.

## Current Canon Details

- Neon signs and billboards.
- Steel towers.
- Glowing data streams.
- Rooftops.
- Dark alleys.
- Converted warehouse hideout.
- Holographic or virtual learning interfaces.

## Districts

### Signal Row

Billboards, antenna towers, message kiosks, public alerts, and city broadcasts. Use for phishing, spoofing, trusted senders, urgent messages, and social engineering stories.

### The Old Stack

Warehouses, old servers, repair shops, backup rooms, and forgotten infrastructure. Use for patching, inventory, legacy risk, backups, and maintenance stories.

### Keylight Gate

Locks, identity badges, public/private keys, signatures, certificates, and access-control metaphors. Use for cryptography, authentication, and trust stories.

### Packet Market

Maker stalls, package tubes, tool exchanges, code crates, and dependency carts. Use for supply-chain trust, provenance, safe tooling, and package-review stories.
