# Ledger Lab

## Summary

The Ledger Lab is a student-facing computing lab attached to the Civic Learning Grid. It tracks shared compute time, storage credits, project queues, lab reservations, and print resources.

## Story Use

Use the Ledger Lab for Season 2's central investigation. It is where the `-0.75` mismatch becomes a real case, where Jinx begins the Threadboard, and where the team learns that shared systems leave records.

## Visual Motifs

- Old terminals beside newer tablets.
- Resource counters.
- Printer trays.
- Project queue boards.
- Storage-credit meters.
- Help desk windows with ticket numbers.

## Canon Notes

- Most Ledger Lab mismatches are ordinary and harmless.
- The lab should not be portrayed as foolish or broken.
- Its weakness is that small anomalies are easy to dismiss because the lab sees noise every day.
