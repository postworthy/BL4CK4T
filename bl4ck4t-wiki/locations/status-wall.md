# Status Wall

## Summary

The Status Wall is the public Cybertropolis board where service state is posted during outages and disruptions.

## Story Use

Use the Status Wall when the city needs to say what is working, degraded, unavailable, unknown, or being handled through a fallback path.

## Visual Motifs

- Four status columns: working, degraded, unavailable, unknown.
- Chalk revisions that preserve what changed and when.
- Small pins from the Service Map.
- Calm public language written under pressure.

## Canon Notes

- The Status Wall is not the Notice Wall. The Notice Wall carries public claims and rumors; the Status Wall carries service-state communication.
- Whiskers should use the Status Wall to practice honest uncertainty.
- The wall should never pretend certainty just to calm people.
