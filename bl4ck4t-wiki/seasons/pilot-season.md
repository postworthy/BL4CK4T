# Pilot Season

## Logline

BL4CK4T tests whether the Script Kitties can see patterns, share secrets safely, and understand how small choices compound over time.

## Existing Episodes

1. The Hidden Code - first BL4CK4T drop and Caesar cipher.
2. Keys To The City - public/private crypto and shared secrets.
3. The Interest Of Time - compound interest and patience.

## Season Theme

- Technical theme: beginner-friendly hidden systems.
- Character theme: the team learns how each member contributes.
- Ethical theme: curiosity should become responsibility.

## Continuity Role

This season can remain standalone. If later tied into the larger series, it should become BL4CK4T's calibration season: three early drops used to learn how the team thinks.
