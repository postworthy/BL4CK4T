# Season 7: The Patch Bell War

## Logline

After Season 6 exposes one old unpatched service, Cybertropolis learns that ignored maintenance can become an emergency inherited by everyone.

## Historical Inspiration

Primary anchor: MSBlaster and Welchia/Nachi.

Contextual background: Code Red, Nimda, and SQL Slammer may be used to establish the early-2000s worm era, patch speed, exposed services, monoculture, and emergency response. They should support the season rather than scatter it.

## BL4CK4T Transformation

The red mark from Season 6 leads to an old City Runtime repair notice. The Runtime is a common operating layer used by desk terminals, service-room machines, public boards, clinic intake stations, library checkout stands, school work surfaces, and permit counters. A known flaw was postponed because the visible systems still worked. Then public boards repaint themselves, desk terminals restart, support queues fill, citizens distrust repair notices, and a Helpful Ghost begins fixing machines without permission.

## Season Theme

- Technical theme: patch debt, worm speed, exposed services, verified repair channels, rollback.
- Character theme: Cipher learns responsible action under bounded uncertainty; Byte learns that clever unauthorized repair is still a violation.
- Ethical theme: good intentions do not erase consent.

## Character Wounds

Cipher is afraid of making a wrong call, so he delays making a necessary call.

Byte is tempted to admire the Helpful Ghost because it works.

Key line to preserve if it fits the eventual story:

> A fix that enters without asking still breaks the door.

## Core Civic Artifact

Patch Bell: a civic signal that rings when a known serious flaw must be patched, isolated, or publicly risk-accepted.

## Core Lessons

- Ignored warnings become inherited emergencies.
- Patch delay is a decision, not a neutral state.
- Emergency action can be responsible without perfect certainty.
- Unauthorized repair can still be intrusion.
- Trusted repair needs verification, consent, rollback, and communication.

## Planned Story Shape

1. A known flaw is discovered in the City Runtime.
2. The Red Clerk insists the update can wait because old runtime repairs once broke something important.
3. The Red Door fever spreads through visible public boards.
4. The team discovers the same runtime also supports desk terminals and service-room machines.
5. Desk terminals restart before people can save work.
6. Citizens become afraid of real update notices.
7. The Script Kitties create a trusted Repair Lane.
8. The Helpful Ghost begins repairing without permission.
9. Byte admires the cleverness, then confronts the boundary violation.
10. Cipher learns to act under bounded evidence.
11. The Patch Bell rule is adopted.
12. The season closes with a quiet log showing someone read source plans before the loud crisis.

## Planned Villain Shape

The Red Clerk represents neglect disguised as stability. His human detail is a remembered update failure that made him fear change.

The Helpful Ghost represents help without consent. It may repair real harm, but it crosses boundaries to do so.
