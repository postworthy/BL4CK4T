## Logline

Cybertropolis learns what happens when a digital city is not broken into, but overwhelmed.

## Historical Inspiration

The Estonia cyberattacks.

## BL4CK4T Transformation

City services, schools, banks, and message boards are buried under waves of useless traffic. The Script Kitties learn resilience, redundancy, and crisis communication.

## Season Theme

- Technical theme: availability, DDoS, resilience.
- Character theme: Whiskers learns leadership during public pressure.
- Ethical theme: digital infrastructure is civic infrastructure.

## Core Lessons

- Availability is part of security.
- Redundancy and preparation matter.
- Attribution can be uncertain.
- Public communication is part of response.

## Episode Shape

1. Signal Row slows under a traffic flood.
2. The team learns that nothing was "stolen" but everything is disrupted.
3. Grimalkin builds resilience maps.
4. Whiskers coordinates calm public messaging.
5. The season ends with Cybertropolis redesigning critical services.
