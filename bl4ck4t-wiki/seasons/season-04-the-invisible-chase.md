# Season 4: The Invisible Chase

## Logline

A chase across Cybertropolis forces the Script Kitties to ask whether fear of technical power can distort the truth.

## Historical Inspiration

Kevin Mitnick, Tsutomu Shimomura, and the hacker-manhunt era.

## BL4CK4T Transformation

A rumored shadow-hacker becomes the city's obsession. The Script Kitties investigate social engineering, identity, media fear, pursuit, and proportionality without turning the season into hero worship or panic.

## Season Theme

- Technical theme: identity, access, social engineering, and trace evidence.
- Character theme: Shadow learns the difference between secrecy and trust.
- Ethical theme: invisible technical power creates fear, and fear can mislead.

## Core Lessons

- Social engineering can bypass technical barriers.
- Media stories can simplify complicated reality.
- Accountability and proportionality both matter.
- Public mythology is not the same as evidence.

## Episode Shape

1. A rumor spreads faster than facts.
2. Jinx separates evidence from legend.
3. Shadow follows subtle traces.
4. The team sees how social engineering works without copying it.
5. The season ends with a lesson about power, fear, and responsibility.
