## BL4CK4T Transformation

The Phishmonger returns with a self-sending glitter letter. The team must teach the city how to pause before opening what looks personal and urgent.

## Season Theme

- Technical theme: social malware and unsafe attachments.
- Character theme: Jinx learns when to trust others with a warning.
- Ethical theme: appealing messages can still be harmful.

## Core Lessons

- Curiosity can be exploited.
- Attachments and file appearances can mislead.
- Defaults and user education matter.
- Reporting helps stop spread.
