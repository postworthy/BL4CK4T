# Supply Serpent

## Summary

Supply Serpent coils through Packet Market, swapping labels, forging seals, and sneaking bad parts into trusted-looking crates.

## Public Motif

- Crates with shimmering seals.
- Tool carts with labels that shift when viewed twice.
- Scales shaped like package tags.
- Whispering delivery tubes.

## Personality

Smooth, patient, and persuasive. Supply Serpent prefers borrowed trust to direct confrontation.

## Inspiration Class

Supply-chain compromise, dependency confusion, provenance, signing, package review, and third-party risk.

## Safe Tactic Abstraction

Supply Serpent introduces fictional mislabeled components into city projects. The Script Kitties learn to verify source, seal, version, and permissions before using a tool.

## Defensive Lesson Mapping

- Check provenance.
- Review dependencies.
- Verify signatures and trusted sources.
- Limit permissions.
- Maintain an inventory.

## Episode Hooks

- Packet Market receives two crates with almost identical names.
- A shortcut tool asks for more permissions than it needs.
- Cipher notices a signature seal that does not match the maker's key.
