# Ledgerjack

## Summary

Ledgerjack is a broker figure connected to the Far Relay. Ledgerjack trades in old passes, project notes, and mismatched records. The character should stay indirect: more market shadow than mastermind.

## Public Motif

- Torn ledger tabs.
- Half-erased account stamps.
- Borrowed keys with no doors attached.
- Receipts that do not add up.

## Personality

Ledgerjack is patient, evasive, and transactional. Ledgerjack prefers other people to make noise while the real trade happens elsewhere.

## Inspiration Class

Brokered access, account misuse, stolen-data markets, and adversaries who profit from other people's weak records.

## Safe Tactic Abstraction

Ledgerjack never teaches or demonstrates real intrusion. Public stories show defender-facing effects: odd resource charges, borrowed passes, mismatched access records, and clues moving through the Far Relay.

## Defensive Lesson Mapping

- Preserve records.
- Protect accounts.
- Verify ownership before accusing.
- Report patterns clearly.
- Treat small mismatches as questions worth checking.

## Episode Hooks

- A torn ledger tab appears beside a resource mismatch.
- The Threadboard shows a Ledgerjack mark near several unrelated-looking records.
- Jinx learns that Ledgerjack sells fragments without always knowing what they mean.
