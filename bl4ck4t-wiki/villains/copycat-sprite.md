# Copycat Sprite

## Summary

The Copycat Sprite is not a person. It is the Season 3 incident force: a fictional self-copying lab sprite that leaves its expected sandbox and fills connected rooms with extra markers.

## Role

- Teaches propagation and resource pressure.
- Forces Byte to face builder accountability.
- Gives Grimalkin a response-coordination challenge.

## Public Boundary

Keep it visual and fictional. It should never become a real malware recipe.

## Published Canon Details

- Begins as a toy process connected to the Season 2 finale.
- Appears as extra blue markers and paper-cat-like sprite copies.
- Causes slow rooms, stalled queues, and response labor.
- Closed by containment, restore slips, room checks, and the First Bell Desk.

## Current State

Resolved as an incident force in Season 3. It should not become a recurring villain unless a future story deliberately reuses it as an archived lesson or simulator case.
