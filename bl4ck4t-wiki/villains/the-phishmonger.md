# The Phishmonger

## Summary

The Phishmonger is a Signal Row trickster who sells urgent-looking messages, prize notices, and fake city alerts. The danger is not brute force; it is making a bad choice feel quick and reasonable.

## Public Motif

- Glittering invitations.
- Almost-official badges.
- Hooks made of neon punctuation.
- Message bubbles that rush readers with timers and promises.

## Personality

Charming, theatrical, impatient, and allergic to careful readers. The Phishmonger wants the city to react before it thinks.

## Inspiration Class

Phishing, social engineering, suspicious-message handling, and credential-protection lessons.

## Safe Tactic Abstraction

The Phishmonger sends fictional city messages that look official until the Script Kitties inspect sender marks, links, tone, and context.

## Defensive Lesson Mapping

- Slow down.
- Verify sender and context.
- Do not follow suspicious links.
- Protect credentials.
- Report suspicious messages.

## Episode Hooks

- A fake BL4CK4T drop appears on Signal Row.
- A prize invitation asks the Script Kitties to "confirm" their hideout passphrase.
- City students receive urgent homework alerts from a sender that is one character off.
