# Villain Backlog

## Summary

Villains should be fictional and playful on the public side, while internally drawing from realistic adversary patterns. Do not expose direct real-world APT mappings in public articles unless the user approves a teacher-facing note.

## Active Villain Roster

- [The Phishmonger](the-phishmonger.md) - phishing, social engineering, suspicious messages, sender verification.
- [Patch Phantom](patch-phantom.md) - patching, maintenance, inventory, ransomware prevention.
- [Supply Serpent](supply-serpent.md) - supply-chain trust, provenance, package review.

## Backlog Concepts

### MirrorMoth

- Inspiration class: impersonation, lookalike domains, spoofed identity.
- Public story shape: creates reflections of trusted city signs that are almost correct.
- Lesson payload: authentication, certificates, careful verification, trust signals.

### FogJack

- Inspiration class: botnets, noisy scanning, opportunistic compromise.
- Public story shape: sends a fog of tiny scripted probes through forgotten devices.
- Lesson payload: default passwords, exposed services, segmentation, monitoring.

### CopySpark

- Inspiration class: worm-like propagation and unintended spread.
- Public story shape: a self-copying sprite that multiplies through city signal tunnels after a careless lab boundary fails.
- Lesson payload: safe testing, containment, rate limits, incident coordination.
