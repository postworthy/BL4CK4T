# The Flood Prince

## Summary

The Flood Prince is a Season 6 adversary figure for The Day The City Would Not Answer. The character represents a civic failure around honest status, triage, and resilient service design.

## Public Motif

The motif should be symbolic and readable without copying real historical actors.
