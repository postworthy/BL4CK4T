# Flood Prince

## Summary

The Flood Prince is the Season 6 adversary figure for attention without responsibility. He believes that if nothing is stolen and nothing is rewritten, then interruption is only noise.

## Public Motif

- Paper crowns folded from blank receipts.
- Repeated knocking marks.
- Overfull request baskets.
- A line that keeps asking, `Are you there?`

## Personality

Showy, wounded, impatient, and defensive. He wants the city to notice him and confuses forced attention with importance.

## Inspiration Class

DDoS-style availability disruption, online crowd pressure, public claims during incidents, and attribution uncertainty.

## Safe Tactic Abstraction

The Flood Prince overwhelms fictional civic request paths with repeated harmless-looking questions. Stories must not describe real attack tooling, real services, or replication steps.

## Defensive Lesson Mapping

- Availability is part of trust.
- A blocked service can be real harm.
- Claims are not proof.
- Defenders can prioritize, filter, communicate, and recover without knowing everything yet.

## Episode Hooks

- A public note claims no harm was done because nothing is missing.
- A crown mark appears near a stalled status board.
- The team discovers that some of the noise is mimicry, not proof of one source.
