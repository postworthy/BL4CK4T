## Continuity Pass

Season continuity passes.

- The Echo Grid opens as a mystery and becomes a shared responsibility.
- The Little Blue Pawprint, Crunch Charm, Tonebox, and Hushline each recur with a clear role.
- Signal Row remains the stable district anchor for the season.
- Pixel's sound-led curiosity stays central without giving him permission to misuse what he learns.
- The finale closes the Echo Grid arc before introducing the Season 2 `-0.75` clue.

## Pixel Character Arc Pass

Pixel's arc passes.

The draft set starts with Pixel following sound because it is beautiful and strange. The middle episodes test whether his curiosity will chase spectacle, status, and clever devices. The final episodes move him toward craft: he learns that understanding a hidden rule also creates responsibility for how that knowledge changes the city.

Season endpoint achieved: Pixel still hears the wonder in the system, but he no longer treats wonder as separate from consequence.

## Evidence And Concept Pass

Evidence and concept handling passes.

- In-band signaling is transformed into the Echo Grid's audible control layer.
- Public technical disclosure and rumor spread become zines, stickers, market talk, and copied charm lore.
- Blue-box mechanics become fictional Tonebox behavior at story level only.
- Counterculture is preserved as mixed motive rather than a single moral position.
- The architectural lesson lands through the Hushline, which separates control from casual sound.

The drafts keep the mystique of hidden systems while omitting practical abuse paths.

## Season 2 Cliffhanger Pass

The Season 2 cliffhanger passes.

The `-0.75` discrepancy appears after the Echo Grid story has closed, which prevents the finale from weakening Season 1's resolution. Jinx notices the clue, BL4CK4T frames it as a small thread worth following, and the next season's detective premise is seeded without naming the historical anchor publicly.

## Historical Cleanliness

The public-facing draft layer does not import real historical names, real institutions, or period telecom instructions. Historical facts remain in the historical account and documentary treatment. The fictional season uses transformed equivalents: Signal Row, Echo Grid, Little Blue Pawprint, Crunch Charm, Tonebox, Row Rebels, and Hushline.
