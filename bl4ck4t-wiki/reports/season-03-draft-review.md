## Continuity Pass

Season continuity passes.

- Season 3 picks up the Season 2 toy-process cliffhanger.
- The Ledger Lab, Glass Bureau, and Threadboard continuity remain intact.
- The Old Stack is new lab infrastructure and does not reopen the Echo Grid or Hushline.
- The First Bell Desk complements the Glass Bureau rather than replacing it.
- The Season 4 clue appears only after the Copycat Sprite incident closes.

## Byte Character Arc Pass

Byte's arc passes.

The draft set starts with Byte wanting a cleaner test and ends with Byte carrying a better builder checklist. The season preserves Byte's practical invention ethos while making the lesson sharper: working code is not the same as a ready experiment.

## Evidence And Concept Pass

Evidence and concept handling passes.

- Propagation is transformed into fictional markers and rooms.
- Containment is shown through lanes, signs, and scope control.
- Incident response is shown through roles and update cadence.
- Recovery is shown through restore slips and verification.
- No public draft explains real worm mechanics.

## Season 4 Cliffhanger Pass

The Season 4 cliffhanger passes.

The anonymous caller points toward chase, public fear, and hacker mythology after Season 3's incident is closed. It does not name the real historical anchor publicly.
