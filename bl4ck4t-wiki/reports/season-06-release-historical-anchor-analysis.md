Pass.

## Fidelity Notes

- The fictional season preserves the historical lesson that availability can become a civic security issue without data theft.
- The story keeps disruption serious but avoids claiming complete citywide paralysis.
- The season transforms access restrictions into a fictional outside-gate dilemma while preserving the fairness and communication tension.
- Public episodes do not name real countries, real attackers, real victims, or reproduce attack mechanics.
- The aftermath focuses on durable response habits, matching the historical importance of resilience and policy change.

## Residual Risk

Low. The main simplification is that the fiction compresses many institutions into a smaller set of city artifacts. The core historical ideas remain intact: dependence, disruption, triage, public communication, attribution discipline, and changed practice.
