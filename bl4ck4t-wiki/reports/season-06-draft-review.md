## Quality Gate Notes

- Episode openings were reviewed in order and use distinct postures: waiting room, records room, request token, map revision, status draft, outside reader, villain claim, fallback desk, and aftermath ledger.
- BL4CK4T drops vary by placement and function. They press a question, clarify a distinction, or sharpen a character decision rather than solving the episode.
- Character pressure is episode-specific: Whiskers leads under uncertainty, Pixel learns absence as evidence, Jinx maps dependencies, Cipher protects attribution discipline, Grimalkin builds triage, Byte learns practical response, and Shadow keeps human impact visible.
- Teaching Tie-Ins use the required five-bullet structure and stay tied to each episode's actual story turn.
- Public prose avoids operational attack detail and keeps the Estonia inspiration transformed into Cybertropolis civic infrastructure.
