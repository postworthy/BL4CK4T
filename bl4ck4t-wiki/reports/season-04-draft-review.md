# Season 4 Draft Review

## Scope

This report reviews the complete Season 4 draft set before and during promotion to public blog files. It checks the nine mission packets and nine article draft mirrors against the historical account, documentary treatment, story arc, and production bible.

## Result

Pass.

## Review Notes

- The season preserves the historical spine at the level of public mythology, pursuit, trace evidence, social engineering, legal/process friction, and proportionality.
- Public fiction transforms the real-world case into Cybertropolis entities and does not use real historical actors as characters.
- Shadow's arc moves from guarded observation to trusted witness.
- Jinx keeps claim classification and knowledge-state discipline visible.
- The Vanishing Caller remains human-scale and accountable.
- The City Chronicle is fast and flawed, not malicious.
- The Season 5 clue appears after Season 4 closure.
