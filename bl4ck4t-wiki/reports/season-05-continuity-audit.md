## Continuity Notes

- The Glitter Letter incident is closed; future use should reference lessons, artifacts, or aftermath rather than reopening the same outbreak.
- The Quarantine Tray can recur as a review artifact for suspicious messages.
- The Message Office is a delivery and reporting institution, not a replacement for the Glass Bureau or First Bell Desk.
- The Season 6 empty-receipt flood should lead into availability, DDoS-style pressure, and civic resilience.
