# Season 5 Draft Review

## Scope

This report reviews the complete Season 5 draft set before and during promotion to public blog files. It checks the nine mission packets and nine article draft mirrors against the historical account, documentary treatment, story arc, and production bible.

## Result

Pass.

## Review Notes

- The season preserves the historical spine at the level of a familiar-looking message, trust-based spread, deceptive appearance, contact-path amplification, response friction, public warning quality, and changed practice.
- Public fiction transforms the real-world event into Cybertropolis entities and does not use real historical actors as characters.
- Pixel's arc centers curiosity with restraint rather than curiosity erased.
- Jinx's evidence discipline remains consistent with Seasons 2 and 4.
- Whiskers' leadership becomes more communication-aware.
- The Phishmonger returns as a style and lure adversary, not an omnipotent cause of every message problem.
- The Season 6 clue appears only after Season 5 closure.
