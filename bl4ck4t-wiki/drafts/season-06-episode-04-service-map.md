Promoted to `content/blogs/season-06-episode-04-service-map.md`.

## Story Draft

Jinx builds a clean Service Map and Whiskers spots its flaw: it lists systems but omits people. The map becomes a civic artifact that tracks impact, dependencies, fallback paths, and waiting citizens.

## Teaching Tie-In

- Concept: dependency mapping.
- Story idea: the Service Map changes when people are added to it.
- Key distinction: mapping systems is not the same as mapping impact.
- Defensive habit: identify critical dependencies and affected people before a crisis.
- Season thread: availability is part of trust.
