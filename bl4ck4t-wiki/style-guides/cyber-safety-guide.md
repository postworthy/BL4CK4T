# Cyber Safety Guide

## Summary

BL4CK4T content is for learning, defense, and ethical curiosity. Public stories should avoid actionable abuse while still teaching real concepts.

## Public Content Rules

- Prefer defensive framing.
- Use toy examples, fictional systems, lab environments, or classroom-safe simulations.
- Avoid real target names unless discussing already-public historical context at a high level.
- Avoid credential theft, persistence, evasion, exploitation chains, or malware instructions as operational steps.
- Include consent and responsible-use framing when tools or tactics appear.
- For advanced topics, explain the idea, impact, and defense before mechanics.

## Internal Wiki Handling

The wiki may preserve real-world source context and TTP mappings for story development, but public articles should transform them into fictional equivalents and age-appropriate lessons.
